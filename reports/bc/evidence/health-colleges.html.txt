class="pageTitle__title"><h1>Colleges, Boards and Commissions</h1></div></div></div></div><span class="last_Updated_Text"><img src="/icons/last-updated-star.svg" alt=""/> <!-- -->Last updated on June 28, 2024</span><div class="topicContent__main"><div id="introduction" class="styled-h2"><p>This section contains relevant information regarding regulatory colleges, boards or commissions established by statute or regulation associated with professional regulation and oversight.</p></div><div id="body" class="styled-h2"><h2>Regulatory Colleges</h2>

<p>The following health profession regulatory colleges have been delegated the authority under provincial legislation to govern the practice of their registrants in the public interest. Their mandate at all times is to serve and protect the public.</p>

<p>The primary function of the colleges is to ensure their registrants are qualified, competent and following clearly defined standards of practice and ethics. All colleges administer processes for responding to complaints from patients and the public and for taking action when it appears one of their registrants is practicing in a manner that is incompetent, unethical, illegal or impaired by alcohol, drugs or illness.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="https://www.bccnm.ca/" target="_self">British Columbia&nbsp;College of Nurses and Midwives</a></li>
	<li><a href="https://oralhealthbc.ca/" target="_self">British Columbia College of Oral Health Professionals&nbsp;</a></li>
	<li><a href="https://www.cchpbc.ca" target="_self"><span lang="EN-US"><span>College of Complementary Health Professionals of British Columbia</span></span></a></li>
	<li><a href="https://www.chcpbc.org" target="_self"><span lang="EN-US"><span>College of Health and Care Professionals of British Columbia</span></span></a></li>
	<li><a href="http://www.bcpharmacists.org/" target="_self">College of Pharmacists of British Columbia</a></li>
	<li><a href="https://www.cpsbc.ca/" target="_self">College of Physicians and Surgeons of British Columbia</a></li>
</ul>

<h2>Boards and Commissions</h2>

<p>Boards and commissions currently operating are described below.&nbsp;These public bodies are established by statute or regulation and are mainly appointed by the Minister or Order in Council.</p>

<h3>Community Care and Assisted Living Appeal Board</h3>

<p>The Community Care and Assisted Living Appeal Board is a tribunal created under the <a href="http://www.bclaws.ca/EPLibraries/bclaws_new/document/ID/freeside/00_02075_01" target="_self"><em>Community Care and Assisted Living Act</em></a> to hear and decide appeals from licensing, registration and certification decisions about community care facilities, assisted living residences&nbsp;and early childhood educators.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="http://www.ccalab.gov.bc.ca/" target="_self">Community Care and Assisted Living Appeal Board</a></li>
</ul>

<h3>Emergency Medical Assistants Licensing Board</h3>

<p>The Emergency Medical Assistants Licensing Board is responsible for examining, registering and licensing all emergency medical assistants in B.C., including first responders. The board, under the authority of the <a href="http://www.bclaws.ca/EPLibraries/bclaws_new/document/ID/freeside/00_96182_01" target="_self"><em>Emergency Health Services Act</em></a>, sets licence terms and conditions. In addition, the board investigates complaints and conducts hearings where necessary.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="/gov/content/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/partners/colleges-boards-and-commissions/emergency-medical-assistants-licensing-board" target="_self">Emergency Medical Assistants Licensing Board</a></li>
</ul>

<h3>Health Professions Review Board</h3>

<p>The Health Professions Review Board is an administrative tribunal created under the <em><a href="http://www.bclaws.ca/EPLibraries/bclaws_new/document/ID/freeside/00_96183_01" target="_self">Health Professions Act</a></em> to provide an independent review of certain decisions made by the self-governing colleges of designated health professions regarding the registration of their members and the timeliness and disposition of complaints made against their registrants.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="http://www.hprb.gov.bc.ca/" target="_self">Health Professions Review Board</a></li>
</ul>

<h3>Hospital Appeal Board</h3>

<p>The Hospital Appeal Board is an independent, quasi-judicial appeal tribunal created under the <a href="http://www.bclaws.ca/EPLibraries/bclaws_new/document/ID/freeside/00_96200_01#section46" target="_self"><em>Hospital Act</em></a> to provide medical practitioners with an avenue of appeal from hospital board of management decisions affecting hospital privileges.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="http://www.hab.gov.bc.ca/" target="_self">Hospital Appeal Board</a></li>
</ul>

<h3>Medical Services Commission</h3>

<p>The Medical Services Commission manages the Medical Services Plan in accordance with the <a href="/gov/content/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/legislation/medicare-protection-act-and-regulations" target="_self"><em>Medicare Protection Act and Regulations</em></a>. The responsibilities of the commission are two-fold: to ensure that all B.C. residents have reasonable access to medical care; and to manage the provision and payment of medical services in an effective and cost-efficient manner.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="/gov/content/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/partners/colleges-boards-and-commissions/medical-services-commission" target="_self">Medical Services Commission</a></li>
</ul>

<h3>Mental Health Review Board</h3>

<p>The Mental Health Review Board is an independent tribunal created under the <a href="http://www.bclaws.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96288_01" target="_self"><em>Mental Health Act</em></a> to conduct hearings to review and decide whether persons certified / detained at any mental health facility in B.C. should continue to be certified / detained based on criteria in the act. The board&rsquo;s function is to make sure patients who are certified by physicians and detained involuntarily in provincial mental health facilities have access to an objective and timely review process.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="/gov/content/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/partners/colleges-boards-and-commissions/mental-health-review-board" target="_self">Mental Health Review Board</a></li>
</ul>

<h3>Patient Care Quality Review Boards</h3>

<p>The Patient Care Quality Review Boards are six independent review boards created under the <em><a href="http://www.bclaws.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/08035_01" target="_self">Patient Care Quality Review Board Act</a>. </em>They&nbsp;receive and review care quality complaints that have first been addressed by a health authority&#39;s Patient Care Quality Office, but&nbsp;remain unresolved. The boards may make recommendations to the Minister of Health and the health authorities for improving the quality of patient care in British Columbia.</p>

<ul>
	<li><a href="https://www.patientcarequalityreviewboard.ca/" target="_self">Patient Care Quality Review Boards</a></li>
