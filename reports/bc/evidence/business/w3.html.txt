<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The following employers have been formally designated to participate in the West Kootenay Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP). This list is published in accordance with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) requirements and will be updated regularly as new employers receive designation or are de-designated.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Being designated means that these employers:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Have been reviewed and approved to participate in the the West Kootenay RCIP</li>



<li>Have committed to supporting newcomer settlement and integration.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-d1e0c2d5 gb-headline-text">De-designated Employers</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An employer may be de-designated for a number of reasons. In some cases, this is administrative. For example, an employer may choose to voluntarily withdraw from the Pilot, the business may close, or the business may be sold. If a business is sold, the new ownership must meet the requirement of being in continuous, active operation under the same management for at least two years in the community before being eligible for designation.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">An employer may also be de-designated if required program conditions are no longer met. This can include situations where the staff member who completed the mandatory trainings leaves the organization and a new authorized representative has not yet completed the required training.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">More serious reasons for de-designation can include non-compliance with federal or provincial employment laws, violations of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (IRPA) or Regulations (IRPR), fraud or misrepresentation, charging fees to a candidate, or failing to meet program commitments.</p>
