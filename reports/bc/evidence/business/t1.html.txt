    <div id="result-1">
        <h2>If you applied for Express Entry</h2>
        <p>These instructions are for permanent residence applications for the</p>
        <ul>
            <li>Federal Skilled Worker Program</li>
            <li>Canadian Experience Class</li>
            <li>Federal Skilled Trades Program</li>
        </ul>
        <h3>Who can apply for a BOWP</h3>
        <p>To be eligible, you must</p>
        <ul>
            <li>live in Canada (and intend to live outside Quebec) at the time you apply for your BOWP
                <ul>
                    <li>You can leave Canada while we process your application.</li>
                    <li>However, if you leave Canada after your work permit expires, you can’t work until your new
                        application is approved.</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
            <li>either
                <ul>
                    <li>have valid temporary resident status <strong>and</strong> a valid work permit</li>
                    <li>have an expired work permit but <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#maintained-status">maintained
                            your status</a> as a worker</li>
                    <li>be eligible to <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/restore.html">restore
                            your status and get a work permit</a></li>
                </ul>
            </li>
            <li>be the principal applicant on your permanent residence application</li>
            <li>have submitted a <a href="https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=072&amp;top=3">complete
                    application</a> for permanent residence and passed the completeness check
                <ul>
                    <li>Submitting a profile to the pool is not the same as applying for permanent residence.</li>
                    <li>Make sure you understand <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/works.html">how
                            Express Entry works</a>.</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
            <li>have your acknowledgement of receipt letter
                <ul>
                    <li>We send this letter to your account after we get your permanent residence application</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
        </ul>
        <div class="alert alert-warning">
            <h3>If you’re not eligible for a BOWP yet</h3>
            <p>Don’t let your work permit expire while you wait for an invitation to apply. Maintain your status in
                Canada by <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend/apply.html">extending
                    your work permit</a>.</p>
            <p>Your employer may need to get a new Labour Market Impact Assessment or submit a new offer of employment
                for you to do this.</p>
        </div>
        <h3>Supporting documents</h3>
        <p>When you submit your BOWP application, include</p>
        <ul>
            <li>the supporting documents listed in the document checklist</li>
            <li>a copy of your acknowledgement of receipt letter</li>
        </ul>
        <div class="alert alert-warning">
            <p><strong>If you apply online</strong></p>
            <p>Upload your acknowledgement of receipt letter in the “Client information” field.</p>
        </div>
        <h3>How to apply</h3>
        <p>Follow the instructions for extending or changing the conditions on your work permit.</p>
        <p>When you apply, make sure you:</p>
        <ul>
            <li>select “Open work permit” as the type of work permit</li>
