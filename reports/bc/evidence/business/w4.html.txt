<div class="gb-container gb-container-809c25d3 animated slideInUp"><div class="gb-inside-container">

<h1 class="gb-headline gb-headline-4dda3792 gb-headline-text">How to become a Designated Employer</h1>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">West Kootenay RCIP designates employers on an ongoing basis. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Only employers operating in one of the <a href="https://westkootenayimmigration.ca/priorities/#prioritysectors" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">six priority sectors</a> can apply to become designated.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">All employers must go through a mandatory designation process to participate in the West Kootenay Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP). This process ensures only eligible, committed employers can access the pilot.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Note:</strong> Designation does not guarantee that a candidate will receive a community recommendation or permanent residence. Every job offer and candidate must undergo a full review by both the West Kootenay RCIP (community level) and Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (federal level).</p>

</div></div>


<div style="background-color:#eeeeee;padding-left:5%;padding-right:5%;padding-bottom:5%;padding-top:5%" class="wp-block-atomic-blocks-ab-container alignfull no-margin animated slideInUp delay-200ms ab-block-container"><div class="ab-container-inside"><div class="ab-container-content" style="max-width:1000px">
<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-8f761849 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow" style="flex-basis:100%"><div class="gb-container gb-container-9884869c">
<div class="gb-grid-wrapper gb-grid-wrapper-a6d289ba">
<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-9fd30649"><div class="gb-container gb-container-9fd30649">

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-6f85ee51 gb-headline-text">Step 1: Check Employer Eligibility</h2>

</div></div>

<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-0d04fddc"><div class="gb-container gb-container-0d04fddc">

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To qualify, your business or organization must:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Be operating in the West Kootenay RCIP region (<a href="https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?mid=1jk6MYSWU7hSxpi4gPPpaydNAFspnUog&amp;ll=49.684416080435994%2C-117.5392682692379&amp;z=9" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">see map</a>).</li>



<li>Conducting business/ organizational operations in a West Kootenay Priority Sector (<a href="https://westkootenayimmigration.ca/priorities/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">see priorities list</a>).</li>



<li>Have been in continuous, active operation under the same management for at least 2 years.</li>



<li>Be in good standing with employment standards, occupational health and safety laws, and federal immigration regulations.</li>



<li>Be seeking to hire permanent, full-time (minimum 30 hours/week), non-seasonal workers.</li>



<li>Be committed to supporting newcomer settlement in the community.</li>
</ul>

</div></div>
</div>
</div></div>
</div>



<div style="height:100px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>


<div class="gb-grid-wrapper gb-grid-wrapper-63b960b6">
<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-84fcc124"><div class="gb-container gb-container-84fcc124">

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-5cadda02 gb-headline-text">Step 2: Contact West Kootenay RCIP </h2>

</div></div>

<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-38bd733b"><div class="gb-container gb-container-38bd733b">

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once you’ve assessed your eligibility, <a href="https://westkootenayimmigration.ca/contact/" data-type="page" data-id="34" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">contact West Kootenay RCIP</a> to confirm eligibility and get access to the mandatory employer training.  </p>

</div></div>
</div>


<div style="height:100px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>


<div class="gb-grid-wrapper gb-grid-wrapper-0fda6a51">
<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-9099b0fa"><div class="gb-container gb-container-9099b0fa">

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-33a4c4df gb-headline-text">Step 3: Complete Mandatory Training</h2>

</div></div>

<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-7f23f62b"><div class="gb-container gb-container-7f23f62b">

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">To become a designated employer, you must complete the following training through Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC): </p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Intercultural Competency Training</strong> which helps foster a welcoming, inclusive workplace.&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>IRCC Employer Onboarding Training</strong> which covers pilot rules and employer responsibilities</li>
</ul>

</div></div>
</div>


<div style="height:100px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>


<div class="gb-grid-wrapper gb-grid-wrapper-247dd134">
<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-3e604653"><div class="gb-container gb-container-3e604653">

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-90cb402d gb-headline-text">Step 4: Submit Employer Designation Application</h2>

</div></div>

<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-ed2ee90c"><div class="gb-container gb-container-ed2ee90c">

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once you’ve sent West Kootenay RCIP your certificates of completion for both mandatory training courses, you will be sent a link to the Employer Designation Application Form.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Submit the application form to West Kootenay RCIP, including:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Business information (address, CRA number, NAICS code)&nbsp;</li>



<li>Labour needs (job titles, NOC/TEER codes, wages, anticipated start dates)&nbsp;</li>



<li>Evidence of operational history&nbsp;</li>



<li>Description of recruitment efforts&nbsp;</li>



<li>Certificates of completed training&nbsp;</li>



<li>Settlement support commitments</li>
</ul>

</div></div>
</div>


<div style="height:100px" aria-hidden="true" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>


<div class="gb-grid-wrapper gb-grid-wrapper-0909aebf">
<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-ee2f1953"><div class="gb-container gb-container-ee2f1953">

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-a55b928a gb-headline-text">Step 5: Await Assessment &amp; Decision</h2>

</div></div>

<div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-b0bdc5fb"><div class="gb-container gb-container-b0bdc5fb">

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">West Kootenay RCIP will:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Review your application&nbsp;</li>



<li>Confirm that your business meets all requirements&nbsp;</li>



<li>Notify you if additional information is needed&nbsp;</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If approved, you’ll receive designation status and can begin submitting Recommendation Applications for candidates you&#8217;d like to hire.</p>

</div></div>
</div></div></div></div>


<div class="gb-container gb-container-0b4a7762 animated slideInUp"><div class="gb-inside-container">

<h1 class="gb-headline gb-headline-dd4d6141 gb-headline-text">How to Maintain Your Designated Employer Status</h1>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Once designated:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>You must notify West Kootenay RCIP of any changes (e.g. candidate leaves, business changes ownership).&nbsp;</li>



<li>You must submit accurate and complete recommendation applications for each candidate.&nbsp;</li>



<li>You will be subject to annual review, which may include site visits or requests for further proof to confirm continued compliance.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

</div></div>


<div style="height:25px" aria-hidden="true" id="designatedlist" class="wp-block-spacer"></div>


<div class="gb-container gb-container-1f91e122 animated slideInUp"><div class="gb-inside-container">

<h1 class="gb-headline gb-headline-b2226b93 gb-headline-text">Designated Employer List</h1>



<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-4027c7b5 gb-headline-text"><strong>Important Notice for Candidates and Immigration Representatives:</strong></h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Do not contact these employers directly to request a job offer <em>unless </em>a position has been publicly advertised.</strong> Designation does not guarantee that an employer is hiring.</p>
