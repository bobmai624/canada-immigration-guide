</li>
<li>a photocopy of your valid (not expired) passport or travel document</li>

<li><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5487-applying-work-permit-outside-canada.html#documents">certified translations</a> (if your documents aren’t in English or French)</li>

<h2 id="employer-specific-documents" class="mrgn-tp-md">Documents for employer-specific work permits</h2>
<p>If you're applying for an employer-specific work permit, you have to include some additional documents with your application. Your prospective employer needs to give you some of these documents.</p>
<p>You’ll <strong>always</strong> be asked to include the following documents: </p>
<ul>
<li>a letter from your current employer</li>
<li>a reference letter from past employers</li>
<li>proof of past work experience (for example, pay stubs)</li>
<li>proof that you meet the requirements of the job (for example, the license or certification if your job is regulated)
    <ul>
        <li>Upload a blank sheet of paper with “Not applicable” written on it if your job doesn’t require a license or certification</li>
    </ul>
</li>
<li>a résumé</li>
</ul>

<p>These additional documents <strong>listed below</strong> will depend on whether or not your job requires a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA).</p>

<h3>LMIA-required work permits</h3>
<p>If your job requires an LMIA, your employer must give you:</p>
<ul>
<li>a copy of the positive LMIA and LMIA file number</li>
<li>an employment contract</li>
<li>a&nbsp;job offer letter</li>
<li>a <a href="https://www.quebec.ca/en/immigration/work-quebec/temporary-workers/temporary-foreign-worker-program/authorizations/application-temporary-selection" rel="external">Quebec Acceptance Certificate (CAQ)</a> (if you plan to work a <strong>paid</strong> job in Quebec for<strong> more than 30&nbsp;days</strong>)</li>
</ul>

<details>
<summary><strong>Positive LMIA</strong></summary>
<p>A positive LMIA (sometimes called a confirmation letter) will show that:</p>
<ul>
<li>there’s a need for a foreign worker to fill the job</li>
<li>no Canadian worker or permanent resident is available to do the job</li>
</ul>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Job offer letter</strong></summary>
<p>You need a job offer letter if your job&nbsp;requires an LMIA.</p>
<p>A job offer letter is a document that a Canadian employer gives you (as a temporary worker). It explains the details of your job.</p>
<p>Generally, a job offer letter (or “employment letter”) is less detailed than a contract.</p>
<p>The letter includes information about:</p>
<ul>
    <li>your pay and deductions from your pay</li>
    <li>your job duties</li>
    <li>conditions of employment, such as hours of work</li>
</ul>
</details>


<h3>LMIA-exempt work permits</h3>
<p>If your job is LMIA-exempt, your employer must give you:</p>
<ul>
<li>an employment contract</li>
<li>an&nbsp;offer of employment number</li>
</ul>

<details>
<summary><strong>Offer of employment number</strong></summary>
<p>You need an offer of employment number if your job is&nbsp;LMIA-exempt.</p>
<p>Your employer will need to submit an offer of employment in the&nbsp;<a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal.html">Employer Portal</a>. Once submitted, the portal generates an offer of employment number that you need when you apply for your work permit.</p>
<p>The offer of employment number starts with the letter&nbsp;‘<strong>A</strong>’&nbsp;followed by a 7-digit number. You can only apply using this number once. Your employer will need to give you a new number for any future application. </p>
<p><strong>Exceptions</strong></p>
<p>Some LMIA-exempt employers don’t need to submit an offer of employment and won’t have an offer of employment number for you, such as&nbsp;<a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/special-instructions/foreign-mission-government-organization.html">foreign missions or international organizations</a>.</p>
</details>
<div class="well well-rnd brdr-0 mrgn-tp-md">
<h3 class="mrgn-tp-0">Open work permit applicants</h3>
<p>If you’re applying for an open work permit and you’re asked to submit any employment documents, upload a <strong>blank document</strong> with the words “Not applicable”.</p>
</div>

    </div>
</div>
