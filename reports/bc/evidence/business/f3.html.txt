  <h1 property="name" id="wb-cont" class="gc-thickline">Get your language assessed</h1>

<p>You need to have a certain level of English or French to apply for permanent residence. This level depends on the type of job offer you have. To prove your language skills, you must</p>
<ul>
<li>schedule your test with an approved agency</li>
<li>pay the test costs</li>
</ul>
