<h2 id="maintain">Maintain your status</h2>
<p>If you apply to extend or change the conditions of your work permit before it expires, you’re legally allowed to stay in Canada while we process your application.</p>
<p>If your work permit expires while we process your application, <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend/expired-permit.html">find out if you can keep working</a>.</p>



</div>
<div class="reference parbase section"><div class="cq-dd-paragraph"><div class="mwsgeneric_base_html mwsgeneric-base-html parbase">



    <span data-incl-name="trp-maintained-status-eng"></span>
<details><summary><strong>Temporary resident permit applicants do not benefit from maintained status</strong></summary>
<p>Temporary resident permit (TRP) applicants <strong>can’t maintain temporary resident status while their application is being processed</strong>. This applies to both</p>
<ul>
<li>initial TRP applications, and</li>
<li>any other TRP applications</li>
</ul>
</details>


</div>
</div>

<div class="mwsgeneric-base-html parbase section">



    <h3>Date and time we receive your application</h3>
<p>To <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#maintained-status">maintain your status</a>, we must receive your application <strong>before</strong> your work permit expires. We determine the date and time an application is received 2&nbsp;ways:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>If you apply online:</strong> You must submit your application before midnight <a href="https://time.is/UTC" rel="external">Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)</a> on the day your work permit expires.
    <ul>
      <li>Our online application system uses&nbsp;UTC.</li>
      <li>Make sure you know the difference between your local time and UTC.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><strong>If you apply on paper:</strong> We stamp paper applications the day we receive them at the case processing centre.
    <ul>
      <li>If we receive an application after your work permit expires, we’ll count back 7&nbsp;days and use that as the date received.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>
<h2 id="before-extend">Before you extend or change the conditions of your work permit</h2>
<p>Before you apply, make sure <strong>your employer</strong> has <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/hire-temporary-foreign/extend-permit.html">completed all the required steps</a>.</p>
<h3>Labour market impact assessment–required work permits</h3>
<p>Some jobs require a labour market impact assessment (LMIA). Your employer is responsible for submitting the application for an LMIA. In most cases, you should wait to apply for a work permit extension until your employer gives you a positive LMIA decision letter.</p>
<p><a href="/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/labour-market-impact-assessment-processing-times.html" class="btn btn-default mrgn-bttm-md mrgn-tp-md">Check the processing times for LMIA applications.</a></p>
<details>
  <summary id="no-lmia-caq">
    If you don’t have your positive LMIA or CAQ decision letter
  </summary>
	<p>You may be able to submit your work permit extension application while your employer’s application for an LMIA or <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#certificat_acceptation_du_quebec">Quebec Acceptance Certificate (CAQ)</a> is being processed.</p>
	<p>You must meet the following criteria if you want to extend your work permit before you receive your positive LMIA or CAQ decision letter:</p>
	<ul>
		<li>Your work permit expires in two weeks or less.</li>
		<li>Your employer submitted a complete application for an LMIA or CAQ.</li>
		<li>You submit all required documents within <strong>90 days</strong> of when you submit your application.</li>
	</ul>
	<p>If you don’t submit your LMIA or CAQ within 90 days, <strong>your application may be refused</strong> for being incomplete.</p>
	<h3>What to include when you apply for your work permit extension</h3>
	<p>If you don’t have your new LMIA or CAQ, you must include <a href="#proof-CAQ-LMIA">additional documents</a> when you submit your application.</p>
</details>
<h3>Check the expiry date on your passport</h3>
<p>You can’t extend your work permit <a href="https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1504&amp;top=15">beyond the expiry date of your passport</a>. Make sure your passport won’t expire before the requested end date of your permit.</p>


</div>

    <h2 id="how-extend">How to extend or change the conditions of your work permit</h2>
<p>In most cases, you must apply online to extend or change the conditions of your work permit.</p>
<details>
  <summary>
    How applying to extend your work permit online helps you
  </summary>
  <ul>
    <li>You can make sure your <a href="http://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=072&amp;top=3">application is complete</a> before you submit it.</li>
    <li>Unless you’re a post-graduation work permit (PGWP) holder, you’ll get a <a href="#centred-popup3" class="wb-lbx lbx-inline" aria-controls="centred-popup3">work permit extension letter (WP-EXT) that proves you can keep working</a> under the <strong>same conditions as your current work permit</strong>.
      <ul>
        <li>You won’t get this letter if you apply on paper, because we won’t know if your application is complete until we review it.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>There are no courier fees or mail delivery time—we get your application instantly.</li>
    <li>You can quickly submit more documents online (if we ask you to).</li>
    <li>You get updates on the status of your application directly in your online account.</li>
    <li>As of June&nbsp;21, 2024, you can no longer apply for a PGWP at a port of entry (airport, land or marine border) when entering Canada. </li>
  </ul>
</details>


<h3>Compare your work permit expiration date with our processing time</h3>

<p>To avoid delays, you should consider the processing time <strong>for in-Canada work permit applications</strong>. This can help you decide when to apply and increases the chance that we’ll process your application before, or shortly after, your current permit expires.</p>

}"></div>

<p>Send your work permit extension application once you have all your documents ready.</p>


<details>
  <summary><strong>Renew your provincial or territorial and federal identity documents before your current work permit expires </strong></summary>
  <p>If you applied to extend your work permit and then your permit expires, you have</p>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#maintained-status">maintained your status</a>, and</li>
    <li>authorization to work without a permit until we make a decision on your application</li>
  </ul>
  <p>You can keep working under the <strong>same conditions</strong> of your current work permit. If you have an employer-specific work permit, this means having the same</p>
  <ul>
    <li>employer</li>
    <li>job</li>
    <li>work location</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Open work permit holders can still change employers and jobs.</p>
  <h4>Renewing provincial or territorial documents</h4>
  <p>While you’re on <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#maintained-status">maintained status</a>, you won’t be able to renew documents issued by your province or territory, such as your</p>
  <ul>
    <li>driver’s licence</li>
    <li>health card</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Make sure your documents are valid before your work permit expires.</p>
  <h4>Applying for or updating the expiry of your social insurance number (SIN) card</h4>
  <p>While on <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#maintained-status">maintained status</a> (while your work permit is expired), you can’t apply for a SIN card <strong>or</strong> update the expiry date of your current SIN. You can, however, keep working under the same conditions as those on your initial work permit. </p>
  <p>Find out what you need and how to <a href="/en/employment-social-development/services/sin/apply.html">apply for or renew your SIN card</a>.</p>
</details>
<h3>Step&nbsp;1: Make sure you have what you need</h3>
<p>Sign in or create an IRCC secure account to start your application.</p>
<p><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/account.html" class="btn btn-call-to-action">Sign in to start your application</a></p>

<ul>

  <li>a valid <a href="#centred-popup1" class="wb-lbx" aria-controls="centred-popup1">credit</a> or <a href="#centred-popup2" class="wb-lbx" aria-controls="centred-popup2">debit</a> card</li>
</ul>
<details>
  <summary>If you can’t apply online</summary>
  <p>You can submit a paper application if either</p>
  <ul>
    <li>you can’t apply online because of a disability</li>
    <li>there’s a problem with the online application
      <ul>
        <li>In some cases, you may be able to apply online if you follow special instructions.</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>
  <p><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend/alternate-format.html">What to do if you have problems applying online</a></p>

</ul>

<p>We’re prioritizing certain work permit applications.</p>

<details>
  <summary>Provincial nominees in certain specialized health care occupations</summary>
  <p>You may be eligible for faster processing for an employer-specific work permit if you</p>
  <ul>

<p>If you applied to renew your work permit before it expired, you can leave Canada and come back, but there are <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend/travel-outside-canada.html">some things you need to know</a>.</p>
