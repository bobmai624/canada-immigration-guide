    <h1 property="name" id="wb-cont" dir="ltr">Employer Portal user guide</h1>
	
        
			<div><div class="mwsgeneric-base-html parbase section">



    <h2>On this page</h2>
<ul>
  <li><a href="#s01">Introduction</a></li>
  <li><a href="#s02">Sign in</a></li>
  <li><a href="#s03">Review or update your business and primary contact details</a></li>
  <li><a href="#s033">Before you create an offer of employment</a></li>
  <li><a href="#s04">Create an offer of employment</a></li>
  <li><a href="#s05">Submit an offer of employment</a></li>
  <li><a href="#s06">Access your offer of employment queue</a></li>
  <li><a href="#s07">Make changes to your account or get help</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s01">Introduction</h2>
<p>If your company or organization wants to bring a foreign national to Canada to work in a position that's exempt from
  requiring a labour market impact assessment (LMIA), you must provide us with the employment details and pay the
  employer compliance fee (if required) before we can approve the work permit application.</p>

<p>You must submit offers of employment and employer compliance fees through the <strong>Employer Portal</strong>. This
  guide provides instructions on how to</p>
<ul>
  <li>create and submit offers of employment</li>
  <li>pay the employer compliance fee</li>
</ul>

<p>If you don't pay the fee or submit an offer of employment <strong>before</strong> the foreign national submits their
  application, we'll refuse the work permit.</p>

<div class="well">
  <p><strong>Open work permits</strong></p>
  <p>If a foreign national is eligible and applying for an <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/temporary-residents/foreign-workers/eligibility/open.html">open
      work permit</a>, you don't need to submit an offer of employment. An open work permit allows foreign nationals to
    work for any employer.</p>
</div>

<h3>Enrol in the Employer Portal</h3>
<p>If you don't have an account, find out how to enrol in the <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal/enrolment-guide.html">Employer
    Portal enrolment guide</a>.</p>

<h2 id="s02">Sign in</h2>
<p>To sign in to the Employer Portal</p>
<ol>
  <li>go to the <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal.html">Employer
      Portal sign-in page</a></li>
  <li>choose the secure credential you used to create your portal account (<a href="http://www.ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?q=576&amp;t=23">Sign-In Partner</a> or <a href="http://www.ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?q=794&amp;t=23">GCKey</a>)</li>
  <li>sign in using your username and password</li>
</ol>

<p>You may receive an error message that says "Your account is not authorized to access this IRCC portal. Please ensure
  you have chosen the correct link to log in to." If you receive this message, make sure you</p>
<ul>
  <li>clicked the right link on the <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal.html">Employer
      Portal sign-in page</a> and</li>
  <li>enrolled in the right portal</li>
</ul>

<p>Once you're signed in, you'll see the Employer Portal welcome page.</p>

<div class="well">
  <p><strong>Employer Portal tips</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>Don't use your browser navigation buttons (forward and back buttons) when in the portal. Use the portal's
      navigation buttons (previous, next, exit, etc.) to move between sections.</li>
    <li>After 15 minutes of inactivity, the system will time you out. If it times out while you're in the application,
      clear your cache and sign in again.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h2 id="s03">Review or update your business and primary contact details</h2>
<p>Once you sign in to your account, you'll see your company's information in the <strong>Business details</strong> and
  <strong>Business primary contact information</strong> fields. You, as the <a class="wb-lbx lbx-modal" title="PrimaryUser" href="#primaryuser" aria-controls="primaryuser">primary user</a>, provided this information when
  you enrolled the business in the portal. Until the primary user submits the first offer of employment, the
  <strong>IRCC employer number</strong> field and all the fields under <strong>View address details</strong> will stay
  blank.
</p>

<section id="primaryuser" class="mfp-hide modal-dialog modal-content overlay-def">
  <header class="modal-header">
    <h3 class="modal-title">Primary User</h3>
  </header>
  <div class="modal-body">
    <p>A <strong>primary user</strong> is the first person to enrol an employer or branch in the portal. The employer
      primary user is the only user to have full access to the portal. They're responsible for assigning access by
      providing PINs to branches and secondary users.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<div class="alert alert-info">
  <h4>The primary user must first submit an offer before other users can submit offers</h4>
  <p>The primary user can create secondary users or branches within the account, but other users can't submit any offers
    until the primary user submits the first one.</p>
</div>

<p>If you want to change the primary user, there must already be at least one secondary user. Once there's a secondary
  user, <a href="mailto:CIC.EmployerPortal-Portaildelemployeur.CIC@cic.gc.ca">email us</a> to make a secondary user
  become the primary user.</p>

<p>After the primary user submits the first offer of employment in the Employer Portal, all the other business details
  will be listed. You'll then be able to update the</p>
<ul>
  <li>business address</li>
  <li>mailing address</li>
  <li>telephone number</li>
  <li>email</li>
</ul>

<p>To change this information</p>
<ul>
  <li>click on <strong>View</strong> and update your contact information
    <ul>
      <li>You'll be directed to the <strong>Business details</strong> page.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>click on the <strong>Update</strong> button for each field to change your business information</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s033">Before you create an offer of employment</h2>
<p>Make sure that</p>
<ul>
  <li>the temporary foreign worker hasn't been charged any recruitment fees</li>
  <li>the temporary foreign worker hasn't been charged any recruitment fees by anyone acting on your behalf</li>
  <li>you give the temporary foreign worker a copy of an employment agreement in their preferred official language
    (English or French) that
    <ul>
      <li>is signed by both you and the temporary foreign worker</li>
      <li>includes information on their occupation, wages and working conditions</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>the temporary foreign worker is aware of <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/know-your-rights-worker-international-mobility-program.html">their
      rights as a worker in Canada</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 id="s04">Create an offer of employment</h2>
<p>To create an offer of employment under "What would you like to do today?"</p>
<ul>
  <li>click on <strong>Submit an offer of employment</strong> to start creating a new offer <strong>or</strong></li>

<h3>Make sure the offer is complete and accurate</h3>
<p>The information required in these 4 forms must be typed in fully and accurately.</p>

<p><span class="glyphicon glyphicon-remove"></span> <strong>Don't</strong> copy and paste information into any of the
  fields.</p>

<p><span class="glyphicon glyphicon-remove"></span> <strong>Don't</strong> enter messages such as "see attached" in any
  of the fields to refer to documents that might be attached to the work permit application. Only information in the
  offer will be assessed in the event of a <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/hire-temporary-foreign/international-mobility-program.html">compliance
    inspection</a>.</p>

<p><strong>You'll be able to upload one of the following documents before submitting your offer:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>proof that you're eligible for a fee exemption
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Get help with <a href="#s05">the compliance fee</a></strong>.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>an explanation of how you meet the LMIA exemption (optional)
    <ul>
      <li>if you didn't enter the explanation under the <strong>Job details</strong> section or</li>
      <li>if you wish to add additional information to your explanation</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>

<p>If your offer isn't complete, we may ask you to resubmit it. Otherwise, the work permit application may be refused.
</p>

<p>If you make a mistake, and the submitted offer <strong>is matched to</strong> the work permit application in the
  system, you won't be able to change it. In this case, you'll have to resubmit the offer.</p>

<p>If the submitted offer <strong>isn't matched</strong> to the work permit application, you can withdraw it and start a
  new one.</p>

<p>To save time when creating a new offer of employment, you can use the <a href="#s06.6">Create Duplicate</a> button to
  copy the offer that you've withdrawn. Then, correct the mistake, or add the information that was missing from the
  first offer.</p>

<p>Find out more about <a href="#s06.5">making changes to offers</a> you've submitted.</p>

    with the conditions required for hiring a temporary worker</a>.</p>

<h4>Innovation Stream</h4>
<p>If you're hiring a worker under the Innovation Stream, you must meet the following wage requirements to qualify:</p>
<ul>
  <li>The wage you're offering must be at or above the <a href="/en/employment-social-development/services/foreign-workers/median-wage.html">median
      hourly wage by province or territory</a>.</li>
  <li>If the position is under a collective bargaining agreement (CBA), the wage you're offering must meet the
    requirements of the CBA.
    <ul>
      <li>Use the <strong>Additional information</strong> text box to provide more detail and to upload a copy of the
        CBA with the offer of employment.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>If the position is not under a CBA, the wage you're offering must be at or above the prevailing wage.
    <ul>
      <li>Use the <strong>Additional information</strong> text box to provide more details about meeting the prevailing
        wage requirement.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>

<details>
  <summary>Prevailing wage definition</summary>
  <p>Under the International Mobility Program, you must pay the prevailing wage, which is defined as the highest of
    either</p>
  <ul>
    <li>the median wage on <a href="https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/trend-analysis/search-wages">Job Bank</a> or</li>
    <li>the wage that's within the wage range that you're paying your current employees hired for the same job and work
      location who have the same skills and years of experience</li>
  </ul>

  <p>To determine the median wage on Job Bank</p>
  <ul>
    <li>go to <a href="https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/trend-analysis/search-wages">Compare wages on Job Bank</a></li>
    <li>in the <strong>Job search</strong> field, enter the job title or the <a href="https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/?GoCTemplateCulture=en-CA">NOC code</a> (2021 version) that best describes the
      duties and requirements of the position
      <ul>
        <li>The hourly median wage will be listed in the middle column by community or area.
          <ul>
            <li>If the median wage is listed as "n/a," consult the provincial or territorial wage.</li>
            <li>If the provincial or territorial wage isn't available, consult the national wage.</li>
          </ul>
        </li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>

  <p>If the position requires more skills and years of experience than the applicable NOC description, the wages offered
    should reflect these additional requirements.</p>

  <p>To determine the wage rate being offered, we'll consider only <strong>guaranteed wages, which exclude</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>overtime hours</li>
    <li>tips</li>
    <li>benefits</li>
    <li>profit sharing</li>
    <li>bonuses</li>
    <li>commissions</li>
    <li>other forms of compensation, for example, piece work (work paid at a fixed rate based on the amount of work done
      rather than the time required to do the work)</li>
  </ul>
</details>

<div class="well">
  <h4>Prevailing wage review</h4>
  <p>If you're hiring a worker under the Innovation Stream, you'll need to make sure that the worker is paid the
    prevailing wage throughout the employment period. To show this, you'll need to review wages</p>
  <ul>
    <li>on the first day of work and</li>
    <li>annually by January 1</li>
  </ul>

  <p>In the <strong>Additional information</strong> text box in the Employer Portal, paste the following text exactly as
    shown:</p>

  <p>On the first day of work and then annually thereafter, I, the employer, will</p>
  <ul>
    <li>review and increase, if required, the wages offered for alignment with the prevailing wages as defined on IRCC's
      website</li>
    <li>review the wage for alignment with Job Bank updates or any increases to wages offered to current employees hired
      for the same job, work location, skills and years of experience</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h4 id="alternate">Alternate compensation scheme</h4>
<p>Use this field if</p>
<ul>
  <li>there's any variance in wage
    <ul>
      <li>for example, bi-monthly union dues or fringe deductions</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>you wish to name a source, other than your organization, that's paying the temporary foreign worker's wages, such
    as
    <ul>
      <li>a foreign-contracted company</li>
      <li>an educational institution employing researchers or providing internships at off-site locations</li>
      <li>other employers in a tripartite agreement</li>
      <li>another source</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>the temporary foreign worker will be working intermittently in Canada over a span of time
    <ul>
      <li>for example, 2 days every 3 months</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>the working hours are intermittent or variable, or they vary from the amount stipulated in the <strong>Hours of
      work</strong> field
    <ul>
      <li>for example, 40 hours a week, but sometimes 60 hours a week</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>

<p>Provide the specific range of hours that the temporary foreign worker will be working in Canada instead of stating
  <strong>Variable</strong>. For example, the foreign national will work between 40 and 100 hours a week, depending on
  the harvest season or weather.
</p>

<p>If you can't get this information from the organization paying the wages, let us know in this same field.</p>

<p>If you don't have enough space in that field, you can use the <strong>Additional information</strong> text box.</p>

<p>Select <strong>Next</strong>.</p>

<h4>Benefits</h4>

<div class="alert alert-warning">
  <p><strong>Benefits for temporary foreign worker</strong></p>
  <p>Currently, the form doesn't let you indicate "not applicable" (n/a) regarding benefits provided to the temporary
    foreign worker. Even if you're not providing benefits to the temporary foreign worker, you should select one of the
    benefits and then provide information, in the <strong>Additional information</strong> text box, about the benefits
    arrangement in effect for this worker.</p>
</div>

<p>Enter the details about the temporary foreign worker's benefits if they'll have any.</p>

<h4>Type of vacation</h4>
<p>Select whether the vacation will be given in days or whether the temporary foreign worker will be paid a percentage
  for vacation pay.</p>

<p>If you select <strong>Days</strong>, you must be sure to distinguish between weeks and days. For example, if the
  worker will be given 2 weeks' vacation, indicate exactly how many working days will be given (for example, 10 working
  days).</p>

<h4>Other benefits</h4>
<p>Any employer-sponsored benefits that the temporary foreign worker receives must be for the entire time period listed
  on the work permit. If the benefits won't start on the first day of the work permit—or if no benefits will be
  provided in the job offer—include this information in either</p>
<ul>
  <li>the <strong>Other benefits</strong> (if applicable) field in the <strong>Benefits</strong> section or</li>
  <li>the <strong>Additional information</strong> text box</li>
</ul>

<p><strong><a href="#validate">Validate, save, and exit</a></strong> when you've completed the form.</p>

<h2 id="s05">Submit an offer of employment</h2>
<p>When you've completed and validated all 4 forms, submit the offer of employment by following these steps:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Select <strong>Continue</strong>, located on the main offer of employment page, to go to the document checklist.
  </li>

  <li>If you're exempt from paying the employer compliance fee, upload your proof of fee exemption and include your
    proof in the <strong>Supporting documents</strong> section.
    <ul>
      <li>If you said you would be paying the employer compliance fee, you won't need to upload any documents.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>You have the option to upload a document explaining how the job meets the requirements of the LMIA exemption if
    this explanation was not previously provided under the <strong>Job details</strong> section.</li>

  <li>Select <strong>Next</strong>.</li>

  <li>Read the <strong>Statement of privacy</strong> and the <strong>Declaration of employer</strong> on the
    <strong>Your declaration and electronic signature</strong> page.
    <details>
        <summary><strong>Complete the 3 attestations as part of the employer declaration:</strong></summary>
        <ol type="a">
            <li>Before providing us with your offer of employment in the portal, you need to attest that you've
                <strong>already
                    provided</strong> the temporary foreign worker with a signed copy of the employment agreement. You can
                prove
                this with a document that
                <ul>
                    <li>has the same occupation, wages and working conditions as you entered in the Employer Portal</li>
                    <li>is drafted in the temporary foreign worker's chosen official language of Canada</li>
                    <li>is signed and dated by both you and the temporary foreign worker</li>
                </ul>
                <p>The agreement needs to be signed and completed before you enter the information in the Employer Portal.
                    This is
                    to ensure that the employer, the temporary foreign worker and the Government of Canada have the same
                    understanding of the wages, occupation and working conditions for the job.</p>
            </li>
    
            <li>You need to attest that you haven't charged your temporary foreign worker <strong>any</strong> fees except
                for
                those that cover
                <ul>
                    <li>electronic travel authorizations</li>
                    <li>temporary resident visas</li>
                    <li>temporary resident permits</li>
                    <li>work permits</li>
                </ul>
                <p>Temporary foreign workers are expected to pay these fees.</p>
            </li>
    
            <li><strong>Anyone acting on your behalf</strong> <strong>can't charge recruitment fees to the temporary foreign
                    worker</strong>. If you use a recruiter
                <ul>
                    <li>make sure the recruiter is
                        <ul>
                            <li>in good standing</li>
                            <li>registered with the recruiter registry in your province if one exists</li>
                        </ul>
                    </li>
                    <li>sign a contract with the recruiter highlighting that recruitment fees can't be charged</li>
                    <li>clearly communicate to temporary foreign workers that recruiters shouldn't charge fees</li>
                    <li>verify with the temporary foreign workers that the recruiter hasn't charged fees</li>
                </ul>
            </li>
        </ol>
    </details>
  </li>
</ol>



<ol start="6">
  <li>Select <strong>I agree</strong>.</li>

  <li>Type your name in the signature panel, and answer the security question.
    <ul>
      <li><strong>The name in the signature panel must match the name of the user who is submitting the offer of
          employment.</strong> This person does not have to be the primary business contact.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>Select <strong>Sign</strong>.</li>

  <li>Pay the employer compliance fee if you're not exempt.</li>

  <li>Select <strong>Transmit and pay</strong>.
    <ul>
      <li>If you're exempt from this fee, select <strong>Transmit</strong>.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>Read the fees summary page, and select <strong>Transmit and pay</strong> again.
    <ul>
      <li>You'll be directed to the Receiver General's online payment website.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Complete the online payment form.
    <ul>
      <li>Select <strong>Process Transaction</strong>.</li>
      <li>Review and print the transaction receipt. You may need this receipt in the future.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>Select <strong>Return</strong>.
    <ul>
      <li>You'll be redirected back to the Employer Portal.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ol>

<div class="well">
  <p><strong>Keep proof of payment</strong></p>
  <p>Print or screenshot the payment confirmation on the Moneris page. We won't issue any other receipt.</p>
</div>

<h3>If you work for a Canadian federal government department</h3>
<p>You may need to use the Interdepartmental Settlements procedures to pay the employer compliance fee instead of using
  a credit card. In this case, the hiring division or appropriate financial or contracting officer in the department
  must <a href="mailto:IRCC.FINIS-RIFIN.IRCC@cic.gc.ca">send us an email</a> instead.</p>

<p>The email must include the</p>
<ul>
  <li>department number: XXXX</li>
  <li>IS organization code: XXXXX</li>
  <li>reference code: XXXX</li>
  <li>contact: name, telephone number and email address</li>
  <li>temporary foreign worker's name (last name, first name)</li>
  <li>amount: $230</li>
</ul>

<p>You'll receive a receipt number once the settlement is processed. Choose <strong>No, I am exempt from paying employer
    compliance fees</strong>, and upload a copy of the receipt in the <strong>Proof of fee exemption</strong> field at
  the end of the offer.</p>

<p>Select <strong>Exit questionnaire</strong> to return to the portal welcome page.</p>

<h3>After you submit an offer of employment</h3>

<div class="well">
  <p>The employment queue status now says "matched" after your offer of employment is matched with a work permit
    application.</p>
</div>

<p>Once you submit an offer of employment, it will be listed in your <a href="#s06">employment queue</a>.</p>

<p>There's a processing delay of a few minutes in the Employer Portal.</p>

<p>The <strong>Details</strong> column shows you the status of each offer of employment. Right after you submit an
  offer, you may see <strong>Processing submission</strong> in this column. You will know the offer has been
  successfully submitted when the status is updated to <strong>Submitted</strong>, and you see an offer of employment
  number.</p>

<p>How to <strong><a href="#s06.5">make changes to an offer</a></strong>.</p>

<h3>Give the offer of employment number to your temporary foreign worker</h3>
<p>The offer of employment number consists of the letter <strong>A</strong> followed by a 7-digit number. It will appear
  in your <strong>employment queue</strong> shortly after you submit your offer.</p>

<p>If you don't see the employer number, wait 24 hours, then check again.</p>

<p>You <strong>won't</strong> receive an email or approval letter to give to the temporary foreign worker. Instead, you
  must give the offer of employment number to the temporary foreign worker. They need this number to submit their work
  permit application.</p>

<p>Once you provide the temporary foreign worker with the offer of employment number, you have completed your part of
  the work permit process for your employee.</p>

<p>Make sure any other hiring or employment documentation you give the temporary foreign worker matches the terms and
  conditions of employment that are stated in the offer. Relevant documentation includes the employment contract and
  letter of offer, and the details within these documents.</p>

<p><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/hire-temporary-foreign/international-mobility-program/after-hiring.html">What
    to do after you hire a temporary worker</a>.</p>

<h2 id="s06">Access your offer of employment queue</h2>
<p>To view your existing offers of employment</p>
<ul>
  <li>sign in to the Employer Portal and</li>
  <li>select <strong>Access the offer of employment queue</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>From your queue, you can</p>
<ul>
  <li>look at offers of employment that you haven't submitted</li>
  <li>complete unfinished offers</li>
  <li>delete unfinished offers</li>
  <li>withdraw offers you have already submitted (that haven't been matched to a work permit)</li>
  <li>update offers that have not been matched to the associated work permit application in the system</li>
  <li>duplicate offers</li>
  <li>use the search tool to find offers of employment you've already submitted</li>
</ul>

<p>All users who have access to your employer or branch account will be able to see any offers that are in progress or
  that have been submitted. They will also be able to edit offers created by another user until the offer is submitted.
</p>

<p>To access employment offers that haven't been matched in the system, you can use the offer of employment number.</p>

<h3>Complete an offer you started</h3>
<p>Select <strong>Continue application</strong> next to the offer you want to complete.</p>

<p>All users who have access to the same queue can complete offers that have been started. Complete and submit the
  offer.</p>

<p><strong>Submit the offer within 60 days</strong>, or it will automatically be deleted from your queue.</p>

<h3>Delete an offer you started</h3>
<p>Select <strong>Delete application</strong> next to the offer you want to delete.</p>

<p>You'll be asked to confirm that you want to delete this offer.</p>
<ul>
  <li>If you delete an offer by mistake, you can't get it back. You'll need to start a new offer to replace it.</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="s06.4">Withdraw an offer you submitted</h3>
<p>You can withdraw the offer of employment and get a refund of the compliance fee if you no longer want to hire the
  temporary foreign worker.</p>

<p>You can withdraw an offer at any time before we issue the temporary foreign worker's work permit. The steps you need
  to follow to withdraw an offer depend on whether</p>
<ul>
  <li>the offer has been matched to the associated work permit application in the system</li>
  <li>we authorized you to submit the paper IMM 5802 form outside of the portal</li>
</ul>

<h4>If the "Withdraw application" option is available</h4>
<p>If you see <strong>Withdraw application</strong> next to your offer, the offer has not been matched to the work
  permit application.</p>

<ol>
  <li>Select <strong>Withdraw application</strong>.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Continue</strong> to confirm that you want to withdraw the offer.</li>
  <li>Read the confirmation message, and select <strong>Continue</strong> again to return to the <strong>Employment
      queue</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<p>Your offer's status will change to <strong>Withdraw pending</strong>.</p>

<p>It can take up to 24 hours for the offer to be withdrawn.</p>

<p>Once completed, the status will change to <strong>Withdrawn</strong>.</p>

<h4>If the "Withdraw application" option isn't available</h4>
<p>If you don't see <strong>Withdraw application</strong> next to your offer, the offer has most likely already been
  matched to the temporary foreign worker's work permit application. The work permit application is in progress or has
  already been finalized. In this case, you won't be able to withdraw your offer using the portal.</p>

<p>If you still want to withdraw the offer, <a href="mailto:IRCC.EmployerPortal-Portailemployeur.IRCC@cic.gc.ca">email
    us</a>. In your email message, explain that you want to withdraw the offer, and include</p>
<ul>
  <li>the offer of employment number</li>
  <li>the reason you want to withdraw the offer</li>
</ul>

<div class="alert alert-info">
  <p><strong>Don't withdraw matched offers</strong><br>
    If you withdraw an offer that has already been matched, the work permit application will be refused.</p>
</div>

<h4>You received authorization to submit your offer outside of the portal</h4>
<p>If we authorized you to submit your offer using the paper IMM5802 form, <a href="mailto:IRCC.EmployerPortal-Portailemployeur.IRCC@cic.gc.ca">email us</a> to withdraw your offer. In your email
</p>
<ul>
  <li>ensure the subject line follows this format: <strong>Cancel offer submitted on (date) by (employer name) for
      (employee name)</strong></li>
  <li>attach the original offer of employment</li>
  <li>attach the payment receipt</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="s06.5">Make changes to an offer of employment</h3>
<p>The steps you need to follow to change an offer of employment depend on where we are in the approval process.</p>

<p><strong>If we've approved the temporary foreign worker's work permit application</strong></p>
<ol>
  <li>Submit a new offer of employment in the Employer Portal.</li>
  <li>Pay the employer compliance fee again.</li>
  <li>Let the temporary foreign worker know that they must apply for a new work permit.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>If we haven't yet approved the temporary foreign worker's work permit application</strong>, but the
  application has been received and linked to the offer, you can't make changes to that offer. You must do the
  following:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Request that the work permit processing office withdraw your offer of employment and refund the compliance fee.
  </li>
  <li>Create and submit a new offer with the new information.
    <ul>
      <li>You might find the new <strong>Create Duplicate</strong> function quicker when creating a new offer.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ol>

<p><strong>If the temporary foreign worker submitted a work permit application to us</strong></p>
<ol>
  <li>The temporary foreign worker must withdraw their work permit application using our <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/contact-ircc/web-form.html">web form</a>.
    <ul>
      <li>They must include the reason for the withdrawal request.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>The temporary foreign worker needs to refer to the new offer of employment number when applying for a new work
    permit.</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>If you submitted the offer, and the work permit application hasn't been received or linked to the
    offer</strong></p>
<ol>
  <li>In your queue, find the offer you want to edit.</li>
  <li>Select the <strong>Update submitted offer</strong> button.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Update form</strong> to make field changes in the desired web form.</li>
  <li>Once the changes are made, the <strong>Form name</strong> status will change from <strong>Transmitted</strong> to
    <strong>Updated</strong>.
  </li>
  <li>Resubmit the updated offer by selecting the <strong>Continue</strong> button and following the <a href="#s06.5">steps to change an
    offer</a>. You'll automatically bypass the payment page.
    <ul>
        <li>You can change information in the job offer’s fields, including the employee’s name.</li>
    </ul>    
</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="s06.6">Make a duplicate of an offer of employment</h3>
<p>To create multiple offers of employment that contain the same details except for the foreign worker's information,
  you can duplicate an offer.</p>

<ol>
  <li>In your queue, locate the offer (<strong>Submitted</strong> or <strong>Not submitted</strong>) that you want to
    duplicate.</li>
  <li>Select the <strong>Create duplicate</strong> button.</li>
</ol>

<p>The new offer (or duplicate) will copy the <strong>Business information</strong> and <strong>Wage and
    benefits</strong> web forms of the selected offer.</p>

<p>The <strong>Foreign worker</strong> web form and the information about the LMIA-exempt offer of employment in the
  <strong>Job details</strong> web form will have to be re-entered.
</p>

<h3>Use the search tool</h3>
<p>To look at the offers of employment that you've submitted, do the following:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Select <strong>Access the submitted offers of employment</strong> from the <strong>Employment queue</strong>
    screen.</li>
  <li>Fill in the fields as needed.</li>
  <li>Select <strong>Search all offers</strong>.</li>
  <li>Select the correct action next to the offer.</li>
</ol>

<p>Depending on the status of the offer of employment, you may also be able to</p>
<ul>
  <li>duplicate the offer</li>
  <li>withdraw the offer</li>
  <li>update the submitted offer</li>
</ul>

<div class="alert alert-info">
  <p><strong>Be careful with your search criteria</strong><br>
    You'll be able to see only the submitted offers of employment that meet your search criteria.</p>
</div>

<h2 id="s07">Make changes to your account or get help</h2>

<h3>Create a secondary user or a branch account</h3>
<p>To create a secondary user or branch account, follow these steps:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Sign into your <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal.html">Employer
      Portal</a>.</li>

  <li>Under the <strong>Manage access</strong> heading, select either <strong>Create a branch</strong> or <strong>Create
      a secondary user for this account</strong>.
    <ul>
      <li>Both options will generate a PIN.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>

  <li>Provide the PIN to the new user.</li>

  <li>The new user must then <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal/enrolment-guide.html#register">register
      for a GCKey or a Sign-in Partner</a> to enrol in the Employer Portal using the PIN you provided.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Fix a mistake in your business information</h3>
<p>You can change information only in these fields: <strong>Contact email address</strong> and <strong>Preferred
    language of notification</strong>. If you need to change your business name, your business number or the primary
  contact, <a href="mailto:IRCC.EmployerPortal-Portailemployeur.IRCC@cic.gc.ca">email us</a> with <strong>Business
    Information Change</strong> in the subject line. In the email, include</p>

<ul>
  <li>the current incorrect business information</li>
  <li>the updated, correct business information</li>
  <li>your organization's primary contact name and email address</li>
</ul>

<h3>Reset your PIN (if you're using GCKey)</h3>
<p>Before you start, <a href="http://www.cic.gc.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=315&amp;top=23">clear your browser's
    cache and cookies</a>. If you don't, you may encounter errors. Follow these steps to reset your PIN:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Open the <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal.html">Employer
      Portal</a> page.</li>
  <li>Click on <strong>Continue to GCKey</strong>.</li>
  <li>Click on <strong>Sign Up</strong>.</li>
  <li>Accept the Terms and Conditions of Use.</li>
  <li>Create a new username and click on <strong>Continue</strong>.</li>
  <li>Create a new password and click on <strong>Continue</strong>.</li>
  <li>Create new recovery questions and click on <strong>Continue</strong>.</li>
  <li>Add an optional account recovery email, or <strong>Skip</strong> to the next step.</li>
  <li>A message will confirm you've created your new GCKey. Click on <strong>Continue</strong>.</li>
  <li>Choose a method of <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/multi-factor-authentication.html#method">2-factor
      authentication</a>.</li>
  <li>Accept the Terms and Conditions.</li>
  <li>Set up your method of 2-factor authentication.</li>
  <li>Click on <strong>Next</strong> on the <strong>Welcome to the IRCC Portal</strong> page.</li>
  <li>When asked if you received a PIN, click on <strong>Yes</strong>.</li>
  <li>Enter the exact name (<strong>name of the primary user</strong>) you used in your previous account (before
    resetting your PIN). Your new PIN is <strong>XXXXXXXX</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Reset your PIN (if you're using Sign-In Partner)</h3>
<p>If you want to reset your PIN and you use Sign-In Partner, <a href="mailto:IRCC.EmployerPortal-Portailemployeur.IRCC@cic.gc.ca">email us</a> to request a new PIN. Include the
  following information in the body of your email:</p>

<ul>
  <li>your full name</li>
  <li>the business operating name</li>
  <li>the Canada Revenue Agency business number</li>
</ul>

<p>We'll email you a new PIN, which will be valid for 45 days.</p>

<p>After you receive your new PIN, follow these steps to recover your account.</p>

<ol>
  <li>Visit the <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal.html">Employer
      Portal</a> page.</li>
  <li>Click on <strong>Continue to Sign-In Partner</strong>.</li>
  <li>Choose your financial institution. Enter your banking log-in information and click on <strong>Continue</strong>.
  </li>
  <li>Read the IRCC Portal Terms and Conditions. Click on <strong>I Accept</strong>.</li>
  <li>On the <strong>Welcome to the IRCC Portal</strong> page, click on <strong>Next</strong>.</li>
  <li>When asked if you received a PIN, click on <strong>Yes</strong>.</li>
  <li>After confirming that you have a PIN, provide the exact name you originally entered in your account (before
    resetting your PIN) and the new PIN you received by email.</li>
</ol>

<p>After you follow these steps, your account will be recovered and will include a complete list of your previous offers
  of employment and users.</p>

<h3>Get help and support</h3>
<p>If you need help completing a field in the Employer Portal, click on the blue question mark icon next to that field.
</p>

<p>If you're having technical problems, <a href="mailto:IRCC.EmployerPortal-Portailemployeur.IRCC@cic.gc.ca">email
    us</a>. In your email, include the following:</p>

<ul>
  <li>your information, including
    <ul>
      <li>your full name</li>
      <li>the Canada Revenue Agency business number</li>
      <li>the business operating name</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>a description of the issue</li>
  <li>a screenshot (or several screenshots) showing the full browser window of
    <ul>
      <li>the page on which you're having difficulties</li>
      <li>any error messages you've received</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>

<p>You can expect a response from us within 5 business days. If the problem can't be fixed in that time, we'll let you
  know what to do next.</p>
