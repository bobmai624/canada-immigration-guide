  <h1 property="name" id="wb-cont" class="gc-thickline">Optional: Work permit</h1>
  
  <p>If you have applied for permanent residence under the Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP), you may also be eligible for a 2-year work permit. The work permit lets you work while your permanent residence application is being processed.</p>
  <p><strong>This work permit:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>is only for the Rural Community Immigration Pilot</li>
    <li>is valid for 2 years</li>
    <li>only lets you work for the employer who offered you the job</li>
  </ul>
  
  <p>To qualify for a work permit, you must:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>have an eligible offer of employment from your designated employer</li>
    <li>have a recommendation from a participating community</li>
    <li><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada.html#location">be eligible to apply for a work permit</a></li>
  </ul>
  
  
  
  <h2>Work permits for spouses or common-law partners</h2>
  
  <p>Your spouse or <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#common_law_partner">common-law partner</a> can apply for an <a href="https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=177&amp;top=17">open work permit</a> at the same time as you apply for your 2-year work permit.</p>
  <p>Their work permit will only let them work in the same community as you.</p>
  <h2>How to apply for the work permit</h2>
  <p>The application forms and process you need to follow depend on where you’re applying from.</p>

  <h2>Visa office requirements for applicants in Syria</h2>
  <p>Choose one of the following: </p>
  <ul>
    <li><a href="https://ircc.canada.ca/english/pdf/kits/forms/IMM5897E.pdf">Amman Visa office instructions
      (PDF,&nbsp;162&nbsp;KB)</a>&nbsp;March 2015 or</li>
    <li><a href="https://ircc.canada.ca/english/pdf/kits/forms/IMM5900E.pdf">Beirut Visa office instructions
      (PDF,&nbsp;217&nbsp;KB)</a></li>
  </ul>
  <!-- -->
  <p>If your country has additional visa office requirements, you need to follow the instructions in the document. In
    some cases, you need to upload a specific form as part of your online application.</p>
  <h3>Step 4: prepare your answers to get your online checklist</h3>
  <p>After you sign in to your online account</p>
  <ol>
    <li>Select “Apply to come to Canada.”</li>
    <li>Select “Visitor visa, study and or work permit” under the “Determine your eligibility and apply online:”
      heading.</li>
    <li>You’ll be asked some questions. You must answer all questions truthfully. We use your answers to create a
      personalized document checklist for you. To apply for a work permit, you must provide specific answers to
      some questions:
      <ol class="lst-lwr-alph">
        <li>When asked “What you would like to do in Canada?” select “work.”</li>
        <li>When asked “How long are you planning to stay in Canada?” select “Temporary, more than 6 months.” </li>
        <li>When asked “What type of work permit do you want to apply for?” select “a work permit with a labour
          market impact assessment exemption.”</li>
        <li>When asked “What is the status of your job offer?” select, “Temporary—at least one year.”</li>
      </ol>
    </li>
    <li>Once you complete the questionnaire, you’ll receive your document checklist. Follow the instructions on
      screen to complete your application.
      <ol class="lst-lwr-alph">
        <li>When you see a request for a document called “Client information,” you must upload the following
          documents as <a href="https://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=1160&amp;top=23">one
          scanned file</a>
          <ol class="lst-lwr-rmn">
            <li>your community recommendation form</li>
            <li><a href="#step3prog">your additional visa office requirements</a>, if applicable for your
              country</li>
          </ol>
        </li>
      </ol>
    </li>
    <li class="list-unstyled mrgn-tp-lg"><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5487-applying-work-permit-outside-canada.html#5487E4">Use
      the guide</a> if you need help filling out the application form.</li>
  </ol>
  <h2>Applying from inside Canada</h2>
  <h3>Step 1: Make sure you have what you need</h3>
  <p>To apply online, you’ll need</p>
  <ul>
    <li>a scanner or camera to create electronic copies of your documents, and</li>
    <li>a valid <a href="#centred-popup1" class="wb-lbx" role="button">credit</a> or <a href="#centred-popup2" class="wb-lbx" role="button">debit</a> card.</li>
  </ul>
  <div class="alert alert-info">
    <p><strong>Note:</strong> your employer must give you an offer of employment number before you start your work
      permit application.</p>
  </div>
  <h3>Step 2: Create your online account or sign in</h3>
  <p>You need an account to apply online. You can use your account to</p>
  <ul>
    <li>pay your fees</li>
    <li>submit your application</li>
    <li>check your status</li>
  </ul>
  <a role="button" class="btn btn-primary" href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/account.html">Create an
  account or sign in</a>
  <h3>Step 3: Access the application tool and prepare your answers</h3>
  <p>After you sign in to your online account:</p>
  <ol>
    <li>Select “Apply to come to Canada.”</li>
    <li>Select “Visitor visa, study and or work permit” under the “Determine your eligibility and apply online:”
      heading.</li>
    <li>You’ll be asked some questions. You must answer all questions truthfully. We use your answers to create a
      personalized document checklist for you. To apply for a work permit, you must provide specific answers to
      some questions:
      <ol class="lst-lwr-alph">
        <li>When asked “What you would like to do in Canada?” select “work.”</li>
        <li>When asked “How long are you planning to stay in Canada?” select “Temporary, more than 6 months.” </li>
        <li>When asked “Which situation best describes your work situation?” select “I am eligible for a work
          permit through an active pilot program.”</li>
      </ol>
    </li>
    <li>Once you complete the questionnaire, you’ll receive your document checklist. You must upload all of the
      required documents, and pay your fees.
      <ol class="lst-lwr-alph">
        <li>Upload your community recommendation under the “Optional documents section” as the document titled
          “Client information.”</li>
      </ol>
    </li>
    <li class="list-unstyled mrgn-tp-lg"><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5487-applying-work-permit-outside-canada.html#5487E4">Use
      the guide</a>, if you need help filling out the application form.</li>
  </ol>
  <ol start="5">
    <li>After you complete your application, follow the online instructions to submit it.</li>
  </ol>
