            <h1>Out-of-Country Credentials</h1>

            <p><span class="intro">Individuals holding a <a data-id="3624" href="/become-certified/recognized-trade-certificates/" title="Recognized Trade Certificates">recognized credential</a> from outside of Canada are eligible to work in the full scope of the trade in Alberta based on their credential without registering with AIT or having writing an examination.</span></p>
<ul>
<li>Refer to the <a href="/SOURCES/PDFS/forms/Summary_Recognized_Credentials.pdf">Summary of Recognized Credentials <i class="fa-regular fa-file-pdf"></i></a> or use the search tool <a data-id="3624" href="/become-certified/recognized-trade-certificates/" title="Recognized Trade Certificates">here</a> to determine if an out-of-country credential is recognized in Alberta.</li>
<li>If you are unable to determine whether or not a credential is recognized, contact your local <a href="http://tradesecrets.alberta.ca/contact-us/?QUERY=1">Apprenticeship and Industry Training office</a>.</li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h4>Programs for Individuals with Recognized Credentials</h4>
<p>Professionals with a recognized credential may apply for the <span class="p-medium"><a data-id="3551" href="/become-certified/trades-qualifier-programs/qualify-for-alberta-certification/program-details/" title="Qualify for Alberta Certification">Trades Qualifier - Recognized Credential Program</a> </span>to obtain an Alberta qualification certificate and to obtain <a data-id="3990" href="/become-certified/interprovincial-red-seal/" title="Interprovincial Red Seal">Red Seal</a> endorsement if it wasn't obtained in the previous jurisdiction. However, this is <span class="p-medium">not required </span>to work in Alberta.</p>
<hr />
<h4>Non-Recognized Credentials</h4>
<p>Individuals who hold a trade credential from outside of Canada that is <span class="p-medium">not recognized</span> by Alberta may wish to apply for the <span class="p-medium"><a data-id="3559" href="/become-certified/trades-qualifier-programs/qualify-based-on-work-experience/" title="Qualify based on Work Experience">Trades Qualifier - Work Experience Program</a> </span>to have their skills, knowledge, and experience assessed against the standards for certification in an Alberta-designated trade.</p>
