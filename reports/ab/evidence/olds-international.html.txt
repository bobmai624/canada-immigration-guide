            <p><h2><span>Application Process</span></h2>
<p><span>We strongly recommend <strong>applying a minimum of 4-6 months in advance</strong> of the program start date - this allows for application (3-6 weeks) and Study Permit processing. It is important to pay close attention to the</span><span>&#160;</span><span><a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html">Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada website for Study Permit Processing</a></span><span>&#160;</span><span>times.</span></p>
<ol>
<li>
<p><span>Submit an application through&#160;<a href="https://www.oldscollege.ca/admissions/index.html" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Apply Alberta</a></span><span>&#160;</span><span>online and pay the non-refundable application fee of&#160;<strong>$90 (CAD)</strong></span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Submit all supporting documentation</strong><span>&#160;</span>to <a href="mailto:admissions@oldscollege.ca">admit@oldscollege.ca</a>&#160;for a preliminary admission assessment&#160;- include your Alberta Student Number (ASN) created at the time of application.&#160;Original transcripts will be required before a final admission decision and documentation will be issued.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Not submitting all supporting documentation in a timely manner will significantly delay Olds College processing times</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span color="#000000">Refer to the&#160;</span><a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.oldscollege.ca/Assets/external/international/International%2520Document%2520Requirements.pdf&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1623423722744000&amp;usg=AFQjCNHxmT6w5bMiz23VQuJqBVgOrkX2xw" href="../../_media/international-document-requirements1.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank" title="International Document Requirements">International Document Requirements</a><span color="#000000"><span>&#160;</span>for information on how to submit your original documents.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span color="#000000"></span><span>International applicants <strong>must meet the specific admission requirements</strong> for their desired program.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><span><strong>Allow 3-6 weeks for application processing</strong> of a</span><span>&#160;</span><i><span>complete</span></i><span>&#160;</span><span>application. Your Admissions Officer will be in contact with you via e-mail with a decision. <strong>Incomplete applications will take longer to process. </strong></span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span>While you wait, <strong>apply and obtain your temporary visa &amp; study permit</strong>. <a href="https://travel.gc.ca/assistance/embassies-consulates" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Contact the Canadian embassy or consulate</a> in your home country for details.&#160;</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span><strong>Reserve your seat</strong> in the program by paying the $5,000 (CAD) tuition deposit through <a href="https://www.oldscollege.ca/admissions/office-of-the-registrar/tuition-and-fees-payments/payment-methods.html">Pay My Tuition</a></span><span>&#160;</span><span>by the deadline indicated in your Admission Letter.</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span><strong>Send proof of application</strong> for your Temporary Visa/ Electronic Travel Authorization &amp; Study Permit to your Admission Officer by the deadline indicated in your Admission Letter.</span></p>
</li>
</ol>
<h3><strong>Supporting Documentation to Submit</strong></h3>
<p>Certified official documents issued by the high school, college or university sent in a sealed envelope bearing the institution&#8217;s seal, stamp or signature are required.</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>High School and Post-Secondary transcripts</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>English Language Proficiency assessments</p>
</li>
<li><span>Copy of passport information page</span></li>
<li>
<p>If your documents are not in the English language, you must provide a certified English literal translation (word-for-word) with the documents at the time of submission. (We recommend&#160;<a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.scholaro.com/translation/Transcript-Translation&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1623423722744000&amp;usg=AFQjCNH02EGLsC5XtwpsMzj8PhlIh5CuAw" href="https://www.scholaro.com/translation/Transcript-Translation" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Scholaro Transcript Translation</a>)</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Refer to the&#160;<a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.oldscollege.ca/Assets/external/international/International%2520Document%2520Requirements.pdf&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1623423722744000&amp;usg=AFQjCNHxmT6w5bMiz23VQuJqBVgOrkX2xw" href="../../_media/international-document-requirements1.pdf" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank" title="International Document Requirements.pdf">International Document Requirements</a><span>&#160;</span>to learn which documentation to submit. If your country isn&#8217;t listed, contact<span>&#160;</span><a href="mailto:admissions@oldscollege.ca" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">admissions@oldscollege.ca</a>&#160;to determine what specific documents are required to be submitted.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h3>International Documentation Assessment</h3>
<p><span>Before you can receive an offer of admission, your original transcripts/diplomas/examination certificate must be submitted for evaluation. All applicants are assessed based on the completion of education at the senior secondary/high school level. Applicants who have completed education in another country must have graduated with appropriate high school documents and credentials.&#160;</span></p>
<p><span>The transcript assessment will be completed by the Olds College of Agriculture &amp; Technology Admission Office</span></p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><span>Once you have applied, a preliminary admission assessment will be completed and for the purposes of this assessment, scan the documents into one pdf file and e-mail to</span><span>&#160;</span><a href="mailto:admissions@oldscollege.ca">admissions@oldscollege.ca</a><span>. </span></p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><span>If scanned copies are difficult to read because of poor copy quality, they will not be accepted.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><span>The Admission Officer will then request that official transcripts be sent to them by issuing institution or organization, in a sealed envelope.</span></p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><span>If your documents are not in the English language, you must provide a certified English literal translation (word-for-word) with the documents at the time of submission.&#160;</span></p>
</li>
<li>
<p><span>Applicants may be required to submit verification documentation to support their application (e.g. WAEC scratch card/e-pin, Admit Card, etc.)</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><span>Once official documents have been received, and presuming that there is still space in the desired program, an Offer of Admission will be made.</span></p>
<ul>
<li>
<p><span>Delays in receiving official documents may result in being placed on a waiting list if the program reaches capacity before official documentation is received. It is important that international applicants send their official documents when an application is submitted.</span></p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Recognition of Prior Learning</strong></h3>
<p><span>Once you are admitted into an Olds College of Agriculture &amp; Technology program, you can request that an assessment be done on any of your previous post-secondary education to see if you might be eligible for transfer credit towards your new program. You will be required to submit:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><span>A notarized colour copy of your post-secondary transcripts, where a signature and/or official stamp is visible</span></li>
<li><span>Copies of all relevant course outlines or descriptions for the courses you want assessed for transfer credit</span></li>
</ul>
<p><em class="font-small"><strong>PLEASE NOTE:</strong>&#160;All transcripts, course outlines and other supporting documentation must be in English. If your documentation is in another language, you must provide a certified English literal translation (word-for-word) along with a notarized colour copy of the original documents.</em><a href="mailto:international@oldscollege.ca"></a></p>
<p><a class="btn" href="../../current-students/academic-services/recognition-of-prior-learning.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Recognition of Prior Learning</a></p></p>
        </div>
    </div>

<p><strong><span>For more information&#160;on tuition or refunds, please visit our</span><span color="#58595b" style="color: #58595b;">&#160;</span><a data-saferedirecturl="https://www.google.com/url?q=https://www.oldscollege.ca/admissions/office-of-the-registrar/tuition-and-fees-payments/tuition-deposit.html&amp;source=gmail&amp;ust=1726607837054000&amp;usg=AOvVaw1D6A7Q3a_-C_UgePy_EyYk" href="https://www.oldscollege.ca/admissions/office-of-the-registrar/tuition-and-fees-payments/tuition-deposit.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Tuition and Fees Payment</a><span>&#160;page.</span></strong></p></p>
