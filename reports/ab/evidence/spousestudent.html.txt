    <h1 property="name" id="wb-cont"><span class="small">International students</span><br>
Help your spouse or common-law partner work in Canada</h1>



</div>
<div class="mwsgeneric-base-html parbase section">



    
<p>The spouses and common-law partners of <strong>some</strong> international students may be eligible for an open work permit. An&nbsp;<strong>open work permit</strong>&nbsp;lets them work for any employer in Canada, except for one that <strong>either</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>is listed as ineligible on the&nbsp;<a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/employers-non-compliant.html">list of employers who have failed to comply with the conditions</a>, <strong>or</strong></li>
  <li>regularly offers striptease, erotic dance, escort services, or erotic massages</li>
</ul>
<h2>On this page</h2>
<ul>
  <li><a href="#who">Who can apply</a></li>
  <li><a href="#how">How to apply</a></li>
  <li><a href="#extending">Extending an open work permit</a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="who">Who can apply</h2>
<p>Your spouse or common-law partner <strong>must meet the eligibility criteria</strong> listed below to be eligible for an open work permit.</p>
<p id="eligibility">Starting January&nbsp;21, 2025, your spouse or common-law partner may be eligible for an <a href="http://ircc.canada.ca/english/helpcentre/answer.asp?qnum=176&amp;top=17">open work permit</a> if you have a valid study permit <strong>and</strong> you’re either</p>
<ul>
  <li>studying in a <strong>master’s degree</strong> program of 16&nbsp;months or longer</li>
  <li>studying in a <strong>doctoral degree</strong> program</li>
  <li>participating in an <a href="#pilot-programs">eligible program</a>, <strong>or</strong></li>
  <li>studying in one of the following professional degree programs at a university:
    <ul>
      <li>Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS, DMD)</li>
      <li>Bachelor of Law or Juris Doctor (LLB, JD, BCL)</li>
      <li>Doctor of Medicine (MD)</li>
      <li>Doctor of Optometry (OD)</li>
      <li>Pharmacy (PharmD, BS, BSc, BPharm)</li>
      <li>Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM)</li>
      <li>Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BScN, BSN)</li>
      <li>Bachelor of Nursing Science (BNSc)</li>
      <li>Bachelor of Nursing (BN)</li>
      <li>Bachelor of Education (BEd)</li>
      <li>Bachelor of Engineering (BEng, BE, BASc)</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>
<p>If they’re not eligible for this open work permit, they may be able to get <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/need-permit.html">another type of work permit</a>.</p>

<h3>Documents to provide</h3>
<p>Your spouse or common-law partner must provide <strong>one</strong> of the following documents to prove your enrolment in a program of study:</p>
<ul>
  <li>a <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents.html#doc1">valid letter of acceptance</a> from your designated learning institution (DLI)</li>
  <li>a proof of enrolment letter from your DLI</li>
  <li>transcripts from your current program</li>
</ul>
<p>If applicable, they must also provide proof that you’re participating or enrolled in one of the eligible programs, such as</p>
<ul>
  <li>a letter from the province or territory </li>
  <li>a letter of acceptance from a participating DLI indicating admission to the program </li>
</ul>
<p>We’ll also ask them to provide additional documents, such as proof of their relationship to you, the student.</p>
<div class="well well-sm">
  <h3 class="mrgn-tp-0">How long the open work permit will be valid</h3>
  <p>Most of the time, your spouse or common-law partner’s work permit will be&nbsp;valid for the same period of time as your study permit.</p>
</div>
<h2 id="how">How to apply</h2>
<p>In most cases, your spouse or common-law partner must apply online for a work permit.</p>
<h3>Step 1: Make sure they have what they need</h3>
<p>To apply online, they’ll need</p>
<ul>
  <li>a scanner or camera to create electronic copies of their documents, and</li>
  <li>a valid <a href="#centred-popup" class="wb-lbx" role="button">credit</a> or <a href="#centred-popup2" class="wb-lbx" role="button">debit</a> card</li>
</ul>
<h3>Step 2: Read the instruction guide</h3>
<p>Even if they apply online, they should <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5553-applying-change-conditions-extend-your-stay-canada-worker.html">read the instruction guide</a> <strong>before</strong> they complete their application. The guide will explain how to complete each field on the form.</p>
<h3>Step 3: Prepare their answers for the online tool</h3>
<p>Before they can upload their forms, they must answer some questions. We use their answers to create a personalized document checklist for them.</p>
<p><strong>Open work permit applications for spouses or common-law partners of students</strong></p>
<p>Due to a technical issue with online applications, you must use the following instructions to apply for the open permit online:</p>
<ul>
  <li>You must answer <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/come-canada-tool.html">some questions</a> to create a personalized document checklist.</li>
  <li>Answer the following questions in the tool <strong>exactly</strong> as shown based on if:</li>
</ul>
<details>
  <summary><strong>You’re applying from outside Canada</strong></summary>
  <ul>
    <li>When asked: Will you be coming to Canada under an active public policy or special measure announced by IRCC?
      <ul>
        <li>Answer <strong>No</strong></li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>When asked: Are you a <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#spouse">spouse</a>, <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#common_law_partner">common-law partner</a> or <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#dependent_child">dependent child</a> of a person who has or is applying for a work permit?
      <ul>
        <li>Answer <strong>Yes</strong></li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>When asked: Are you a <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#spouse">spouse</a> or <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#common_law_partner">common-law partner</a> of a person who holds a study permit?
      <ul>
        <li>Answer <strong>Yes</strong></li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>When asked: What type of work permit do you want to apply for?
      <ul>
        <li>Answer “<strong>A work permit for a spouse of an international student, or for a family member of a worker</strong>”</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>
</details>
<details>
  <summary><strong>You’re applying from inside Canada</strong></summary>
  <ul>
    <li>When asked: Does one of the following apply to you? I am applying for an open work permit under an active public policy or pilot program announced by IRCC.
      <ul>
        <li>Answer <strong>No</strong></li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>When asked: Are you a <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#spouse">spouse</a>, <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#common_law_partner">common-law partner</a> or <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#dependent_child">dependent child</a> of a person who holds a valid work permit or is applying for a work permit?
      <ul>
        <li>Answer <strong>Yes</strong></li>
      </ul>
    </li>
    <li>When asked: Are you a <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#spouse">spouse</a> or <a href="/en/services/immigration-citizenship/helpcentre/glossary.html#common_law_partner">common-law partner</a> of a person who holds a study permit?
      <ul>
        <li>Answer <strong>Yes</strong></li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>
</details>
<h3>Step 4: Know the fees you have to pay</h3>
<p>In most cases, their fees will include processing fees for them and anyone they include on their application.</p>
<p>We’ll ask them to pay their fees at the end of their application.</p>
<h3>Step 5: Create their online account or sign in</h3>
<p>They need an account to apply online. They can use their account to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>pay their fees</li>
  <li>submit their application</li>
  <li>check their status</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/account.html" class="btn btn-primary">Create an account or sign in</a></p>
<details>
  <summary>
    <h3>If they can’t apply online</h3>
  </summary>
  <p>They can submit a paper application if either:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>they can’t apply online because of a disability</li>
    <li>there’s a problem with the online application</li>
  </ul>
  <p><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/help-your-spouse-common-law-partner-work-canada/alternate-format.html">What to do if they can’t apply online</a></p>
</details>
<h2 id="extending">Extending an open work permit</h2>
<p>Your spouse or common-law partner may be eligible to extend their existing open work permit under this stream if you meet the following requirements:</p>
<ul>
  <li>You have a valid study permit.</li>
  <li>You’re physically in Canada or will physically be in Canada while studying.</li>
  <li>You’re a full-time student enrolled in a <a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html#eligibility">PGWP-eligible program</a> at a designated learning institution (DLI).</li>
  <li>You’re not in the last term of your study program.</li>
</ul>
<p>Additionally, your spouse or common-law partner cannot extend their open work permit under this stream beyond the expiration date of your study permit. </p>
<h3>Documents you need</h3>
<p>Your spouse or common-law partner must give the following documents to prove your enrolment in a program of study leading to a degree:</p>
<ul>
  <li>proof that you’re a full-time student at one of the types of schools listed above, which can include
    <ul>
      <li>a letter of enrolment from your DLI</li>
      <li>transcripts from your current program</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>a copy of your study permit</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend.html" class="btn btn-primary">Extend  your open work permit</a></p>
