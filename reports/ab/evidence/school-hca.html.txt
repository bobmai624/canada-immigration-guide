Having extra credits is going to help me a lot.  </p>    <div class="testimonial-footer">      <p><strong>Nyssa Koleric</strong></p>     <p>Health Care Aide, 2019 </p>   </div>  </div>  </div>  </section><section class="container 1left"><div class="container-grid"><article><h3>What to expect from the program</h3><p>NorQuest College is licensed to deliver the <a class="tool-tip" href="#hca-provincial-curriculum">Health Care Aide Government of Alberta Provincial Curriculum<i class="fas fa-question-circle"></i></a><strong>.</strong></p>

<p>Graduates of the HCA Provincial Curriculum offered through NorQuest will meet the following mandated <a class="tool-tip" href="#hca-program-outcomes">program learning outcomes<i class="fas fa-question-circle"></i></a>&nbsp;which will help them to:</p>

<ol>
	<li>Perform competently within the roles and responsibilities as a HCA within the Alberta and the Canadian health care system.</li>
	<li>Demonstrate professionalism, accountability, and responsibility as a HCA within a collaborative care team.</li>
	<li>Provide ethical person-centred and culturally competent care to support, promote, and maintain the health and well-being, safety, independence, and comfort for the client across the lifespan in all health sectors.</li>
	<li>Apply foundational theory, principles, and evidence-based practices to provide holistic care for clients across the life span and across all health sectors.</li>
	<li>Communicate effectively with the collaborative care team.</li>
	<li>Perform skills competently according to evidence-based guidelines across a variety of care settings for clients with various health needs and conditions.</li>
	<li>Work with the collaborative care team to set and achieve common care goals.</li>
	<li>Maintain safety of self, client, and environment by following government legislation, agency policy, and procedures.</li>
	<li>Respond appropriately by recognizing, acting, reporting, and recording while integrating knowledge, practice, and experience to provide quality care.</li>
	<li>Complete ongoing self-reflection to continuously develop HCA competencies.</li>
</ol></article><article><h3>Graduate with experience</h3><p>NorQuest College believes in the importance of on-the-job experience that provides an enriched learning opportunity.</p>

<p>You&rsquo;ll graduate from this program with<strong>&nbsp;320 hours of work experience</strong>&nbsp;from three practicums.</p>

<h4><strong>Clinical placements</strong></h4>

<p>You will take part in a full-time clinical practicum and must arrange your own transportation to and from the facility. You may need to travel up to 100 km.</p>

<p>You will have  <a class="tool-tip" href="#hca-instructor-placements">two instructor-led clinical placements<i class="fas fa-question-circle"></i></a>&nbsp;and  <a class="tool-tip" href="#hca-preceptored-clinical"> one consolidated clinical placement<i class="fas fa-question-circle"></i></a>.</p></article></div><hr></section>


<section class="container"><div class=""><h3>Selecting your delivery option </h3>

<p>Choose an 8 or 12-month program from the delivery options below. Consider your learning style, flexibility, and preferred location when selecting.</p>

<p>All delivery options include a mandatory in-person lab and clinical component (full days), which may account for 50% of course hours.</p>

<h4>8 months</h4>

<p><strong>In person</strong> - Edmonton Campus with all theory and lab classes on campus during the week (Monday to Friday) and clinicals in Edmonton and surrounding areas. If you attend all your classes in person, at the Edmonton campus, then you will complete your program in eight months when learning full-time.</p>

<p><strong>Online with in-person labs</strong> - Edmonton Campus with theory classes provided online and lab classes (labs on Fridays and Saturdays) on campus and clinicals in Edmonton and surrounding areas. </p>

<p><strong>Wetaskiwin</strong> &ndash; Wetaskiwin Campus with lab classes in the evenings on campus and clinicals in Wetaskiwin and surrounding areas. Theory classes will be online.</p>

<h4>12 months</h4>

<p><strong>Evening, in person</strong> - Edmonton Campus with all theory and lab classes on campus in the evenings and clinicals in Edmonton and surrounding areas. </p>

<p>Once enrolled, you&rsquo;re expected to complete according to your chosen delivery option. Changes may be approved only in extenuating circumstances after meeting with a student program advisor and leadership.</p>

<hr />
<h3>Health Care Aide Workplace Education</h3>

<p>Are you currently working as a health care aide, or in an equivalent care-giving position, and want to become certified? The <a href="/programs-and-courses/programs/health-care-aide-workplace/">Health Care Aide Workplace Education</a> option is offered throughout Alberta in partnership with health-care employers. It can be taken over twelve months, allowing you to study at home and have your skills assessed by a NorQuest College trained tutor at your worksite.</p>
</div><hr /></section>

<section class="container 1left"><div class="container-grid"><article><h3>Additional information</h3>

<p>All health care aides must register with the College of Licensed Practical Nurses and Health Care Aides of Alberta (CLHA) to practice in Alberta. To qualify for registration, you must pass the&nbsp;<a href="https://www.clha.com/hca/apply-to-be-an-hca/registration-information/alberta-hca-registration-examination/" target="_blank&quot;">Alberta HCA Registration Exam</a>.</p>

<p>Visit the <a data-mce-href="https://www.clha.com/hca/apply-to-be-an-hca/registration-information/" href="https://www.clha.com/hca/apply-to-be-an-hca/registration-information/" target="_blank"><strong>CLHA website</strong></a>&nbsp;for further details on registration.</p></article></div><hr></section>
<section class="container 1left"><div class="container-grid"><article><h3>Career opportunities</h3><p>Health care aides work under the direct or indirect supervision of a regulated health professional, such as a nurse, in a variety of settings including:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Assisted living facilities</li>
	<li>Home care</li>
	<li>Long-term care</li>
</ul>

<p>Upon successful completion of the program, you will be prepared to work in continuing care institutions, home care agencies, group homes, assisted or supportive living facilities, day programs, specialized care centres and programs, acute care hospitals, and private care.</p>

<p>For more information on related careers see:&nbsp;</p>

<ul>
	<li><a data-mce-href="https://alis.alberta.ca/occinfo/occupations-in-alberta/occupation-profiles/health-care-aide/" href="https://alis.alberta.ca/occinfo/occupations-in-alberta/occupation-profiles/health-care-aide/" target="_blank">ALIS OCCinfo occupational profiles</a></li>
	<li><a data-mce-href="https://alis.alberta.ca/occinfo/occupations-in-alberta/occupation-profiles/health-care-aide/%22%20/l%20%22wage-salary" href="https://alis.alberta.ca/occinfo/occupations-in-alberta/occupation-profiles/health-care-aide/%22%20/l%20%22wage-salary" target="_blank">ALIS WAGEinfo salary info</a></li>
</ul></article><article><h3>What to expect as a health care aide</h3>

<p>Health care aides provide basic health services, assistance, and support with daily living activities for clients who have medical conditions or major functional limitations.</p>

<p>Applicants should assess their ability to fulfill the demands of the health care aide occupation.</p>

<p>As a health care aide, you must be able to:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Work day, evening, and night shifts any day of the week</li>
	<li>Be on your feet for extended periods of time</li>
	<li>Bend, squat and lift up to 40 pounds to move clients who have minimal body strength</li>
	<li>Function effectively in stressful situations</li>
	<li>Perform all skills independently</li>
</ul></article></div><hr></section>

<script type="text/javascript">
    let programIntakesRecords = [];
    let todosPages = [];
    let selectedPage;
    let selectedIndex;
    let startIndex;
    let endIndex;
    let pagingList;
    let pagesize = 8;
    var rangestarter = 1;

    var myProgram = document.getElementById('programAbbrSelector').getAttribute('data-abbr');
    let url = "/norquestcollege_program/getprogramintakes?programAbbr=" + myProgram;

    fetch(url, {
        method: 'post',
        headers: {
            'Content-Type': 'application/json'
        },
        //make sure to serialize your JSON body
        body: JSON.stringify({
            programAbbr: myProgram
        })

    })
        .then(response => response.json())
        .then(json => {

            programIntakesRecords = json;

            //paging
            let pagesNumber = programIntakesRecords.length / 8;
            const pagesNumberRemainder = pagesNumber % 1;
            const pagesNumberWithoutRemainder = Math.floor(pagesNumber);
            if (pagesNumberRemainder > 0)
                pagesNumber = Number(pagesNumberWithoutRemainder) + 1;
            todosPages = Array(pagesNumber)
                .fill(1)
                .map((x, i) => i + 1);
            startIndex = 0;
            endIndex = pagesize;
            selectedPage = 1;
            nextPage = selectedPage + 1;

            if (programIntakesRecords.length > 0) {

                //fill the paging list
                injectPagination();

                //start mapping the list
                injectPage();
            }
            else {
                for (let el of document.querySelectorAll('.PaginationIntakes')) el.style.display = 'none';
            }

            if (programIntakesRecords.length < 8) for (let el of document.querySelectorAll('.PaginationIntakes')) el.style.display = 'none';


        });

    injectPagination = () => {

        let html = "";
        //html = `<li class="page-item"><a class="page-link" href="javascript:void(0)" onclick="goPrevious()">***1** Previous</a></li>`;
        for (let page = selectedPage; page <= (programIntakesRecords.length / 8) + 1; page++) {

            //    html += `   <li class="pager__item pager__item--"><a class="page-link" href="javascript:void(0)" onclick="goPrevious()">++9966 ${page}</a></li>`;

            //html += `<li class="pager__item pager__item--next page-item">
            //                <a title="Go to next page" class="page-link" href="javascript:void(0)" onclick="goNext()">
            //                    Next

            //                </a></li>`;

            let previuosLink = " ";
            let nextLink = " ";


            if (programIntakesRecords.length > 8) {
                if (selectedPage > 1) {
                    previuosLink = ` <li class="pager__item pager__item--previous"><a class="page-link" href="javascript:void(0)" onclick="goPrevious(1)"> « Previous</a></li>`;
                }

                if ((selectedPage * 8) < (programIntakesRecords.length)) {
                    nextLink = `<li class="pager__item pager__item--next page-item"><a title="Go to next page" class="page-link" href="javascript:void(0)" onclick="goNext()"> Next »</a></li>`;
                }
            }

            pagingList = previuosLink + printNavigation(selectedPage) + nextLink;
        };

        document.getElementById("paging").innerHTML = pagingList;

        document.getElementById("totalPages").innerHTML = programIntakesRecords.length;
        var rangeEnd = programIntakesRecords.length;

        if (selectedPage != 1)
            rangestarter = startIndex + 1;
        else
            rangestarter = 1;

        if (endIndex < rangeEnd)
            rangeEnd = endIndex;

        document.getElementById("pagesrange").innerHTML = (rangestarter + " - " + rangeEnd);
    };

    injectPage = () => {

        const list = programIntakesRecords
            ? programIntakesRecords
                .slice(startIndex, endIndex)
                .map(
                    (programItem, index) =>

                        `
                                                                                                                 <a OnClick="setYear('${programItem[0]['yearTitle']}','${programItem[0]['monthTitle']}','${programItem[0]['year']}','${programItem[0]['abbr']}','${programItem[0]['intakeid']}');" tabindex='0'>
                                                                                                    <div class="program-tile">
                                                                                                         <h4>${programItem[0]['monthTitleDisplaylbl']} ${programItem[0]['yearDisplaylbl']}</h4>
                                                                                                              <div class="program-tile-info">
                                                                                                                <div>
                                                                                                                  <p><small>Location</small></p>
                                                                                                                  <p><b>${programItem[0]['location']}</b></p>
                                                                                                                </div>
                                                                                                                <div>
                                                                                                                  <p><small>Delivery</small></p>
                                                                                                                  <p><b>${programItem[0]['delivery']}</b></p>
                                                                                                                    ${printIntakeTimeofDay(programItem[0]['timeofday'])}
                                                                                                                </div>
                                                                                                              </div>

                                                                                                              <p><small>Statuses</small></p>
                                                                                                                    <div class="program-statuses">
                                                                                                                    ${printIntakeStatus(programItem[0]['demoesticStatus'], 'Canadian / PR')}
                                                                                                                    ${printIntakeStatus(programItem[0]['IndigenousStatus'], 'Indigenous')}
                                                                                                                    ${printIntakeStatus(programItem[0]['internationalStatus'], 'International')}
                                                                                                                    </div>
                                                                                                            </div>
                                                                                                                  </a>          `
                )
                .join("") //to prevent unexpected comma
            : "";
        document.getElementById("list").innerHTML = list;
    };
    //__________________________________________________________________________________________

    goPrevious = (selectedCountPage) => {
        if (selectedPage != 1) {
            startIndex -= pagesize * selectedCountPage;
            endIndex -= pagesize * selectedCountPage;


            selectedPage--;
            nextPage = selectedPage + 1;
            injectPage();
            injectPagination();
        }
    };

    //__________________________________________________________________________________________
    goNext = () => {
        if (selectedPage < todosPages.length) {

            startIndex += pagesize;
            endIndex += pagesize;



            selectedPage++;
            nextPage = selectedPage + 1;
            injectPage();
            injectPagination();
        }
    };

    //__________________________________________________________________________________________
    goDoubleNext = (count) => {
        if (selectedPage < todosPages.length) {

            startIndex += pagesize * count;
            endIndex += pagesize * count;


            selectedPage = count;


            nextPage = count + 1;
            injectPage();
            injectPagination();
        }
    };
    //__________________________________________________________________________________________
    goPage = (pageNo) => {
        if (pageNo == 1)
            startIndex = 0;
        else {
            startIndex = (pageNo * pagesize) - pagesize;

        }
        endIndex = startIndex + pagesize;

        if (selectedPage <= todosPages.length) {
            selectedPage = pageNo;
            nextPage = selectedPage + 1;
            injectPage();
            injectPagination();
        }
    };
    //__________________________________________________________________________________________

    printNavigation = (selectedPage) => {

        if (selectedPage <= todosPages.length) {
            var nav = '';
            for (let i = 1; i < todosPages.length + 1; i++) {
                if (i == selectedPage)
                    nav += `<li class="pager__item is-active"><a href="#" title="Page"><span class="visually-hidden">Page</span> ${i} </a>  </li>`;
                else
                    nav += `<li class="pager__item "><a title="Go to page" class="page-link" href="javascript:void(0)" onclick="goPage(${i})">${i}</a></li>`;
            }
            return nav;
        }
    };

    //__________________________________________________________________________________________

    function printIntakeStatus(statusType, intake) {
        // alert(statusType);
        if (statusType == '<span class="NA">NA</span>')
            return '';
        else
            return '<p><span>' + intake + '</span > ' + statusType + '</p > ';
    }

    function printIntakeTimeofDay(timeOfDayintake) {
        // alert(timeOfDayintake);
        if (timeOfDayintake != '')

            return '<p><small>Time</small></p> <p><b>' + timeOfDayintake + '</b></p>';

        else
            return '';

    }

    function convertFetch(data) { // copy from Shaun See you here

        // data = data.replace('<string xmlns="http://tempuri.org/">[', '');
        // data = data.replace(']</string>', '');

        data = JSON.parse(data);
        return data
    }

    function setYear(yeartitle, monthTitle, year, programAbbr, intakeid) {

        // document.getElementById('startDateID').innerHTML = yeartitle;

        var url = "/norquestcollege_program/setprogramdatabyyear";

        fetch(url, {
            method: 'post',
            headers: {
                'Accept': 'application/json',
                'Content-Type': 'application/json'
            }, body: JSON.stringify({
                academicyear: yeartitle
                ,
                programAbbr: programAbbr
                ,
                year: year,
                monthTitle: monthTitle
            })

        }).then(response => response.text())
            .then(data => {
                if (data == '"Ok"') {
                    // alert(returnData);
                    window.location = location.protocol + '//' + location.host + location.pathname + '?year=' + yeartitle + '&term=' + monthTitle + '&intakeid=' + intakeid;
                }
            });
    }
