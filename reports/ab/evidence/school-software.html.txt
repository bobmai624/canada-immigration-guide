<h2>Credit credentials</h2>
<p>A credit credential includes courses with post-secondary credit applied.</p>
<p>These credits will be used towards your specific program and may be accepted towards a credential at SAIT or a different post-secondary institution.</p>
<p>The credit credentials include a bachelor's degree, applied degree, diploma, certificate and post-diploma certificate.</p>
<h3>Bachelor Degree</h3>
<p>SAIT has bachelor's degree programs in business, construction, and hospitality and tourism management.</p>
<p>Bachelor's degrees are generally four-year programs, but you have up to 10 academic years to complete your courses.</p>
<h3>Applied Degree</h3>
<p>SAIT's full-time applied degree programs involve one year of study through classes and lab work followed by one year of directed studies in a workplace setting. This builds on the completion of a recognized two-year diploma, which you must have to be admitted to an applied degree program.</p>
<p>You then have seven academic years to finish your applied degree, starting from the day you begin the first course in that program.</p>
<h3>Diploma</h3>
<p>At SAIT, over 40 full-time diploma programs cover a broad range of areas, from aircraft maintenance to travel and tourism.</p>
<p>Most diploma programs are completed in two years, though you can take up to seven academic years to complete the requirements.</p>
<h3>Certificate</h3>
<p>SAIT has several certificate programs in the sciences, health and trades.</p>
<p>These programs provide the essential skills to start a career but are shorter in length than SAIT's diploma programs - usually a year or less. However, you have up to five years to complete your program.</p>
<h3>Post-Diploma Certificate</h3>
<p>These programs build on skills developed through a diploma or degree program and combine classroom instruction with a workplace practicum.</p>
<p>They are often completed in a year or less.</p>
<h2>Journeyperson</h2>
<p>A certified journeyperson is recognized as a qualified and skilled person in a trade and is entitled to the wages and benefits associated with that trade. You must complete all apprenticeship periods and pass the exams to earn a journeyperson designation.</p>
<h2>Non-credit credentials</h2>
<p>A non-credit credential comprises courses and programs designed for professional or personal development.</p>
<p>Because these do not have post-secondary credits attached, they cannot be used towards any of the credentials above. We also cannot guarantee an external institute will accept these courses or credentials for transfer credit.</p>
<p>SAIT's non-credit certificates of achievement and completion are offered through <a href="https://coned.sait.ca/?_ga=2.177933410.610757512.1619619812-1978246797.1611070629" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Centre for Continuing Education and Professional Studies">Continuing Education and Professional Studies</a>.</p>
<h3>Certificate of Achievement</h3>
<p>Certificates of achievement recognize the completion of a program that includes a formal evaluation of performance and a minimum of 144 (required and elective) course hours.</p>
<p>Certificates of achievement typically develop broader, complex skill sets. As of July 1, 2022, you have up to three years to complete the certificate. If you began these programs before July 1, 2022, you still have five calendar years to complete the program from when you began the first course.</p>
<h3>Certificate of Completion</h3>
<p>Certificates of completion recognize the completion of a program that includes a formal evaluation of performance and a minimum of 30 to a maximum of 143 course hours.</p>
<p>These certificates are shorter in duration than certificates of achievement and offer a focused education program. As of July 1, 2022, you have up to three years to complete the certificate. If you began before July 1, 2022, you will have five calendar years to complete the program from when you began the first course.</p>
<h3>Professional Certificate</h3>
<p>Program certificates recognize completion of a program that includes the formal evaluation of performance, and that is a minimum of one year in length.</p>
<p>These certificates have program-specific admission requirements.</p>
<h3>Micro-credential</h3>
<p>A micro-credential recognizes the completion of a non-credit course that includes a formal performance evaluation and verifies you have demonstrated specific competencies.</p>
<p>Micro-credentials focus on the mastery of specific skills. You receive a digital, shareable badge upon completing a SAIT micro-credential (SAITMicro).</p>

<h2>Program length</h2>
<p>Estimated program length if you study full-time (nine or more credits per semester.) Actual program length will vary based on the number of courses you are registered in and successfully complete per semester.</p>
<p>You must complete your credential within the timeframes listed below: </p>
<ul>
<li>Applied degree: up to seven academic years to complete all courses.</li>
<li>Diploma: up to seven academic years to complete all courses.</li>
<li>Certificates: up to five academic years to complete all courses.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Location</h2>
<p>The location (also known as delivery mode) is where your classes will take place. For programs with multiple locations, some delivery options may not be offered in every intake or semester.</p>
<ul class="list-unstyled">
<li><strong>In-person (Campus name):</strong>
<p>You'll take all your classes in person on the specified campus.</p></li>
<li><strong>Online:</strong>
<p>You'll take all your classes online.</p></li>
<li><strong>Blended (Campus name):</strong>
<p>You'll take some classes in person at the specified campus and others online.</p></li>

<section aria-labelledby="overview-heading" class="overview-wrapper" id="overview">
<h2 id="overview-heading">
                                    Overview
                            </h2>
<p>Our Software Development diploma program equips you with the skills and knowledge required to become a versatile full-stack developer. No background in software development or engineering is needed.</p><p>You will learn to work on front-end and back-end components of software systems, including web, mobile and desktop applications. You'll become a collaborative team player and critical thinker who has what it takes to stay current with industry trends.</p><p>As a student, you will:</p><ul><li>develop secure and user-centric software solutions by integrating user requirements effectively</li>
<li>gain expertise in the software development lifecycle, including designing, modelling, testing and implementing software solutions</li>
<li>learn to recognize and apply software architectures, design patterns, frameworks and emerging technologies in software development</li>
<li>make decisions that impact scalability and maintainability</li>
<li>learn the basics of software development platforms, operating systems and cloud-based infrastructure</li>
<li>develop troubleshooting skills, essential to diagnose problems and implement effective solutions</li>
<li>refine your business communication skills and work ethic </li>
<li>cultivate professionalism, social responsibility and ethical awareness to thrive in the world of technology</li>
<li>collaborate with stakeholders to complete a project and problem-solve to meet their needs.</li>
</ul><p>Software developers play an integral role in managing and improving business processes, making decisions that shape how users interact with products, interfaces and applications. </p><p>As a graduate, you will be ready for a variety of career and professional development opportunities. Job opportunities include junior software developer or programmer, software tester, quality assurance specialist,  database administrator, web developer, or desktop support specialist.</p><p>Become part of the next generation of software development professionals. With this diploma, you can contribute practical knowledge and business value to the ever-evolving tech landscape. </p>
</section>

<h2>Fees</h2>
<p>Your fees are calculated based on the number of course credits you take per semester (approximately 16 weeks of study). In most cases, your fee is determined based on whether you're taking <strong>more</strong> or <strong>less</strong> than nine (9) course credits (more than nine credits in a semester are considered full-time studies).</p>
