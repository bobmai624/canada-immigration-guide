Job offer in a regulated occupation in Alberta and holds 
certification or licensing demonstrating meets regulatory 
requirements to practice the occupation in Alberta 
 
Some professional occupations and designated occupations and 
trades are regulated occupations in Alberta. Professional occupations 
are regulated by professional regulatory bodies and designated trades 
and occupations are regulated by Alberta Apprenticeship and Industry 
Training (AIT).  
 
Only those who have an Alberta job offer in a regulated occupation 
and have a recognized certification or licensure from the professional 
regulatory body responsible for the occupation in Alberta will be 
awarded points. 
 
Claiming Points for Regulated Trades under the AAIP  
 
To claim the 10 points for working in a regulated occupation on your 
AAIP Worker Expression of Interest (WEOI) application, you must hold 
one of the following:  
• A valid Alberta Qualification Certificate, or  
• A trade certificate recognized by Alberta Apprenticeship and 
Industry Training (AIT).  
 
All designated or regulated trades are eligible to claim the 10 points for 
working in a regulated occupation, as long as the individual holds one 
of these certificates.  
 
If certification is not compulsory in your occupation and you do not 
have a trade certificate, you cannot claim points under regulated 
occupation.  
 
Maximum Points: 10
