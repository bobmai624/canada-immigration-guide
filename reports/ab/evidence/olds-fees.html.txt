                            <h2 class="accordion__title">
                                <button aria-controls="accordion__content-1-1" aria-expanded="false" class="accordion__button" id="accordion__button-1-1">Tuition and Fee Definitions</button>
                            </h2>
                            <div class="accordion__content accordion__content--hidden" id="accordion__content-1-1">
                                <div class="accordion__content__inner wysiwyg"><h3>Olds College Fees</h3>
<p><strong>Tuition</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">: Fees Olds College has been authorized to collect by the Provincial Government under the Post-Secondary Learning Act [Sec. 61(1)] for the use of instruction in courses that are part of programs approved by Alberta Advanced Education (AAE) under the Program of Study Regulation (AR 91/2009).</span></p>
<p><strong>Program Fee:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Fees Olds College collects to cover the costs of materials and supplies that facilitate instruction in a program that are not included in tuition.</span></p>
<p><strong>Field Trip Fee:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> To cover the travel costs for field trips.</span></p>
<p><strong>Health and Wellness Service Fee: </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">To provide services to students in the areas of nursing and healthcare, counselling, and health promotion and education.</span></p>
<p><span><strong>Indigenous Service Fee:</strong>&#160;</span><span>To provide students with opportunities to learn about Indigenous ways of knowing including participating in traditional, cultural and knowledge sharing activities and events.</span></p>
<p><strong>Registrar&#8217;s Office Service Fee: </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">to provide services to students in the areas of student academic advising, student funding and awards, work integrated learning, academic records, convocation events and orientation.</span></p>
<p><strong>Information Technology (IT) Service Fee: </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">To provide information technology (IT) services to students in the areas of printing, learning management system, Classroom A/V Equipment, lab and Learning Commons computers, internet and wifi, and student facing IT operational and software costs</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">.</span></p>
<p><strong>Library Service Fee: </strong><span style="font-weight: 400;">To provide access to students in the areas of accessibility services, help desk and the learning commons, library collections, tutoring and learning coach services.</span></p>
<p><strong>Athletics and Recreation Fee:</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> To provide services to students in the area of athletics, complimentary game day experience, recreational and fitness programming opportunities, access to the Fitness Centre, and access to the Aquatic Centre.</span></p>
<h3>Students' Association (SAOC) Fees</h3>
<p><strong>Students&#8217; Association of Olds College (SAOC) Operations Fee:</strong> T<span>his fee is used to support a variety of&#160;</span><span>student focused services and initiatives such as student advocacy, health and wellness events, student&#160;</span><span>scholarships &amp; awards, academic support, volunteerism &amp; leadership opportunities, The Crossing Restaurant and Pub, Bloom Cafe, student clubs, The SAOC Pantry, marketing &amp; communications, maintenance and upkeep of student spaces, and daily operational costs.</span></p>
<p class="font-small"><em>**Exclusion: this fee is not charged to students enrolled in Dual Credit, or students enrolled in Continuing Education non-PAPRS approved credentials</em></p>
<p><strong>Health and Dental Benefit Plan:&#160;</strong><span>The SAOC offers an extended Health and Dental plan for full-time&#160;</span><span>students that provides coverage for one year after commencement. Students with existing health and&#160;</span><span>dental benefit plans, may complete a form to opt out of this fee.&#160;</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span>This fee is not charged to students enrolled in Heavy Equipment Operator, Pre-Employment programs, Apprenticeship programs, Applied Degree DFS, Dual Credit, and Continuing Education non-PAPRS approved credentials - students in these programs may inquire about opting in to the plan by contacting the SAOC Student Benefits Plan Office.</span></p>
<p dir="ltr"><span>For information regarding this plan, please contact&#160;<a href="mailto:oldsplan@mystudentplan.ca" rel="noopener" target="_blank">oldsplan@mystudentplan.ca</a>&#160;or 403 556-4626.</span></p></div>
