<h1 class="page-heading" id="page-title">
                               English Proficiency
                            </h1>
<div aria-label="Details" class="banner-details">
<p>At SAIT, you must demonstrate English language proficiency. English is the language of instruction for all programs at SAIT and is the most common language used in business in Alberta and Canada as a whole.</p>
<p>SAIT has the right to use discretion in determining adequate levels of English language proficiency to ensure an applicants success in the program.</p>

<p>You must demonstrate English language proficiency in one of the following ways:</p>
<p>1. Submit official transcripts or a completed international document assessment verifying completion of one of the following steps:</p>
<ul>
<li>At least three years of full-time study taught in English a minimum of 12 hours per week at an English speaking secondary school.</li>
<li>At least two years of post-secondary education taught in English at a recognized institution.</li>
<li>Successfully complete a recognized credential from a SAIT-approved post-secondary institution program must have been taught in English.</li>
<li>Review our list of <a href="/assets/documents/about-sait/policies-and-procedures/academic-student/ac-1-5-1-schedule-a-sait-approved-english-language-proficiency-exempt-countries-and-institutions.pdf">SAIT-Approved English Language Proficiency Exempt Countries and Institutions</a> (AC.1.5.1 Schedule A) to learn if education completed in your home country satisfies our English Proficiency requirement.</li>
</ul>
<p>2. Successfully complete a post-secondary credit course in English, comparative literature or communications or equivalent at SAIT or a recognized post-secondary institution minimum C grade required.</p>
<p>3. Achieve the applicable score on an English assessment test the test score must be within one year prior to the date you submit your application.</p>
<p>4. Successfully complete both COMN155 and SPCH155 in the <a href="https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/english-language-foundations">English Language Foundations (ELF) program</a>.<a href="https://www.sait.ca/programs-and-courses/english-language-foundations"></a></p>
<p>5. Successfully complete one of the recognized alternatives to our English Language Foundations program listed below.</p>
</div>
</div>
<h3><a id="alternatives"></a>Recognized alternatives to English Language Foundations</h3>
<p>The following programs are recognized alternatives to our ELF program:</p>

<h2 class="h3 my-0" id="land-acknowledgement-title">Oki, Âba wathtech, Danit'ada, Tawnshi, Hello.</h2>
<p class="my-0">SAIT is located on the traditional territories of the Niitsitapi (Blackfoot) and the people of Treaty 7 which includes the Siksika, the Piikani, the Kainai, the Tsuut&#8217;ina and the &#206;y&#226;rhe Nakoda of Bearspaw, Chiniki and Goodstoney.</p>
<p class="my-0">We are situated in an area the Blackfoot tribes traditionally called Moh&#8217;kinsstis, where the Bow River meets the Elbow River. We now call it the city of Calgary, which is also home to the M&#233;tis Nation of Alberta.</p>
