<h3>Permanent consultation process for the NOC 2031 and beyond</h3>

<p><a href="https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/consultation/2024/noc">Invitation to participate in the revision of the National Occupational Classification (NOC)</a> <strong>Updated on: </strong>October 28, 2025</p>

<p><a href="https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/consultation/2024/noc/results-report">Revising the National Occupational Classification (NOC) 2021 Version 1.0 to NOC 2026 Version 1.0: What we Asked, What we Learned and What we Did Report</a> <strong>Updated on: </strong>October 28, 2025</p>
</div>

<h2>Status</h2>

<p>This standard was approved as a <a href="https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/subjects/standard/napcs/notice/compulsory">departmental standard</a> on August 10th, 2021.</p>

<h2>NOC 2021 Version 1.0</h2>

<p>The publication of the National Occupational Classification (NOC) 2021 is the thirtieth anniversary of the standard occupational classification system and it introduces a major structural change. The NOC 2021 Version&nbsp;1.0 overhauls the "Skill Level" structure by introducing a new categorization representing the degree of Training, Education, Experience and Responsibilities (TEER) required for an occupation. The NOC 2021 Version&nbsp;1.0 also introduces a new 5-digit hierarchical structure, compared to a 4-digit hierarchical structure in the previous versions of the classification. The NOC has been developed and maintained as part of a collaborative partnership between Employment and Social Development Canada and Statistics Canada. This revision is extensive; the last structural revision was NOC 2011.</p>
