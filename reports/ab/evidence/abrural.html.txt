  <h2>Overview</h2><p>This page includes eligibility requirements for applicants who have been offered a full-time job in a Designated Community and have an Endorsement of Candidate Letter from the Designated Community.</p><ul><li>Applicants to the Rural Renewal Stream may be temporary workers residing in Canada with a valid work permit or individuals who are not in Canada.</li></ul><p>The Rural Renewal Stream has a 2-step process:</p><ol><li>Once a community is designated by the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP), the community follows one of these endorsement processes:<ul><li>A Designated Community directly recruits foreign workers to meet labour needs and provides an Endorsement of Candidate letter to selected candidates.</li></ul></li><li>Candidate submits a Worker Expression of Interest. If selected by <abbr>AAIP</abbr>, the candidate will be invited to submit an application.</li></ol>

  </div>

  
	<div class="paragraph paragraph--type--call-out paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100 goa-callout-template--standard">
					<div class="goa-callout goa--standard goa-callout-full">
								
  <p>Read the details on <a href="/aaip-rural-renewal-stream-community-designation" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="4822c3b4-2541-4d9f-84bc-ba651cd7ad37" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Rural Renewal Stream – Community designation">community requirements for designation and the endorsement process</a>.</p>

							</div>
			</div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h2>Requirements</h2><p>Read the requirements to understand which apply to you. Some requirements may differ for temporary workers and individuals not living or working in Alberta or Canada.</p><p>Rural Renewal Stream criteria applies at the time your application is submitted and at the time the AAIP assesses your application. Review the criteria carefully to understand eligibility requirements.</p><h3>Community residency requirement</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted and at the time AAIP assesses your application, you must intend to reside and work in your Designated Community in Alberta or are already living and working in your Designated Community.</p><h3>Ineligible applicants</h3><ul><li>Refugee claimants already in Canada.</li><li>Foreign nationals living in Canada who do not have a valid work permit.</li></ul><h3>Community endorsement requirement</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted and at the time AAIP assesses your application, you must have a valid Endorsement of Candidate letter issued by the Economic Development Organization (EDO) of your designated rural community in Alberta. The recommendation must be based on criteria established by the EDO. In addition, you must meet AAIP Rural Renewal Stream eligibility criteria.</p><h4>Endorsement of Candidate letter</h4><p>All candidates must have an Endorsement of Candidate letter from their Designated Community. Endorsement of Candidate letter is only valid for one year from the date of issuance.</p><p>The letter must be issued by the local EDO of the community and must outline the following:</p><ul><li>date of issuance</li><li>name and address of the EDO supporting the application</li><li>your full name and residential address</li><li>details of the bona fide job offer:<ul><li>name of the business</li><li>name and contact information of the employer</li><li>location of employment</li><li>position and description of job duties</li><li>duration of employment and work conditions</li></ul></li><li>detailed description of how filling the position you are offered aligns with the economic growth of the designated community</li><li>your employer’s efforts to fill the position with Canadian citizens, permanent residences or youth within the community, and outcomes</li><li>name, title and contact information of the person issuing the Endorsement of Candidate letter</li></ul><h3>Occupation requirements</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted, and at the time AAIP assesses your application, you must have a job offer in an eligible occupation.</p><p>An occupation is considered eligible if it is not on the list of in-eligible occupations in Table 1 below. Use <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/eligibility/find-national-occupation-code.html" target="_blank">IRCC’s Find your NOC tool</a> to find the NOC 2021 version of your occupation.&nbsp;</p><p>Most occupations under NOC TEER categories 0, 1, 2, 3,4 and 5 are eligible under AAIP with the following conditions:</p><ul><li>Eligible for TEERs 0–5:<ul><li>Temporary workers who reside in Alberta and hold a valid work permit.</li></ul></li><li>Eligible for TEERs 0–3 only:<ul><li>Temporary workers who reside outside Alberta but within Canada and hold a valid work permit.</li><li>Individuals who reside outside Canada.</li></ul></li></ul><h3>Ineligible occupations</h3><p>All individuals with job offers for the following occupations at the time of application submission and assessment are <strong>not eligible</strong> to apply for or be nominated under the Rural Renewal Stream.</p><p><strong>Table 1. </strong>List of ineligible occupations</p><div class="goa-table"><table><caption>*This National Occupation Classification (NOC) code consists of both eligible occupations and ineligible occupations. The ‘Occupation’ column specifies which occupations are ineligible for each NOC Code listed.</caption><thead><tr><th class="goa-table 20">NOC Code (2021)</th><th class="goa-table 20">NOC TEER category</th><th class="goa-table 60">Occupation</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>00010</td><td>0</td><td>Legislators</td></tr><tr><td>60040*</td><td>0</td><td>Escort agency managers, massage parlour managers</td></tr><tr><td>41100</td><td>1</td><td>Judges</td></tr><tr><td>51111</td><td>1</td><td>Authors and writers (except technical)</td></tr><tr><td>51122</td><td>1</td><td>Musicians and singers</td></tr><tr><td>42200*</td><td>2</td><td>Justices of the peace</td></tr><tr><td>53121</td><td>3</td><td>Actors, comedians and circus performers</td></tr><tr><td>53122</td><td>3</td><td>Painters, sculptors and other visual artists</td></tr><tr><td>53124</td><td>3</td><td>Artisans and craftspersons</td></tr><tr><td>53200</td><td>3</td><td>Athletes</td></tr><tr><td>33100*</td><td>3</td><td>Dental laboratory bench workers</td></tr><tr><td>44100</td><td>4</td><td>Home child care providers</td></tr><tr><td>44101</td><td>4</td><td>Home support workers, caregivers and related occupations</td></tr><tr><td>64321</td><td>4</td><td>Casino Occupations</td></tr><tr><td>55109</td><td>5</td><td>Other performers</td></tr><tr><td>65229</td><td>5</td><td>Other support occupations in personal services</td></tr><tr><td>85101</td><td>5</td><td>Harvesting Labourers</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Job offer requirements</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted, and at the time AAIP assesses your application, you must have a full-time job offer or employment contract from an Alberta employer to work in an eligible occupation within an established business operation in your Designated Community.</p><p>Alberta job offers or employment contracts and the Alberta employer offering the job must meet all the requirements outlined on <a href="/job-offer-and-employer-requirements" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="77f3cc47-5941-41bf-9f13-efd28c2f7257" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Job offer and employer requirements">Alberta Job Offer and Employment Requirements</a>.</p><p>You are responsible for working with your Alberta employer to ensure that all job offer or employment contract and employer requirements are met. Your application will be declined if these requirements are not met. Your application fee will not be refunded where an application is declined based on Alberta employer requirements not being met.</p><h4>Ineligible applicants based on job offers</h4><p>The following individuals are not eligible to apply for or be nominated under the Rural Renewal Stream, even if they have a job offer to work 30 hours a week or more in a 12-month period:</p><ul><li>part-time, casual or seasonal employees, regardless of their working hours</li><li>independent contractors, business owners or temporary agency workers, including individuals listed as directors, shareholders or agents of the Alberta employer on the Corporate Registry System (CORES)</li><li>employees who work in Alberta in a place of employment that is not zoned for commercial or industrial operations, such as a home-based business</li><li>employees who do not work on premises in Alberta, such as those who work in a ‘virtual’ location or serve the employer by telecommuting from a location outside Alberta</li><li>foreign nationals in Canada without a valid work permit</li></ul><h3>Qualifying work experience requirements</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted, you must have a minimum of 12 months full-time work experience in an eligible occupation within the last 18 months prior to your application. This work experience can be a combination of experience gained in Alberta, in Canada (outside Alberta) or abroad.</p><p>For all applicants, work experience in the qualifying period must:</p><ul><li>have been paid full-time employment, for a minimum of 30 hours a week</li><li>meet the TEER category requirements</li><li>meet the occupation requirements above</li></ul><p><strong>Table 2</strong>. Job offer and work experience TEER Categories.</p><div class="goa-table"><table><thead><tr><th>TEER Category of job offer or current occupation is:</th><th>TEER Category of work experience must be:</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>TEER 0</td><td>TEER 0 or 1</td></tr><tr><td>TEER 1</td><td>TEER 0, 1 or 2</td></tr><tr><td>TEER 2</td><td>TEER 1, 2, 3 or 4</td></tr><tr><td>TEER 3</td><td>TEER 1, 2, 3 or 4</td></tr><tr><td>TEER 4</td><td>TEER 2, 3, 4 or 5</td></tr><tr><td>TEER 5</td><td>TEER 5 <strong>and</strong> must be in the same occupation – NOC code</td></tr></tbody></table></div><ul><li>have been authorized by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) and with valid temporary resident status if obtained in Canada</li><li>not have been gained while studying in Canada and doing co-op work placements or internships as part of a study program, unless you are a PGWP holder who completed paid co-op work terms as a part of your program of study at an Alberta post-secondary institution. To qualify, the work terms must have been:<ul><li>paid and full-time (minimum 30 hours per week), and</li><li>for work experience all gained in Alberta</li></ul></li></ul><p>Post-Graduation Work Permit holders who have completed a 2-year post-secondary education program at an IRCC designated learning institution in their Designated Community are not required to meet the 12 months of work experience criterion.</p>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Licensing, registration and certification requirements</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted and at the time AAIP assesses your application, you must meet the following licensing, registration and certification requirements:</p><ul><li>You must have the required licensing, registration or certification to work in your current occupation in Alberta or occupation in your Alberta job offer. Information on the occupation specific requirements is on the <a href="https://alis.alberta.ca/occinfo/" target="_blank">Alis website</a>.</li><li>If you are working or have a job offer in a <a href="https://tradesecrets.alberta.ca/trades-in-alberta/compulsory-certification-trades" target="_blank">compulsory trade</a> in Alberta, you must have a valid <a href="https://tradesecrets.alberta.ca/become-certified/out-of-province-certificates/recognized-trade-certificates" target="_blank">Alberta Apprenticeship and Industry Trade</a> (AIT) recognized trade certificate.</li><li>If you are working as childcare worker in Alberta, you must have valid certification as a Level 2 Early Childhood Educator (formerly Child Development Worker) or Level 3 Early Childhood Educator (formerly Child Development Supervisor) prior to applying to AAIP.</li></ul>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Language requirements</h3><p><strong>Important</strong>: You must confirm the TEER Category for your job offer/occupation in the <a href="https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/Home/" target="_blank">NOC 2021 version</a> and use the TEER Category to determine the minimum language score using Table 3 below.</p><p>At the time your application is submitted, you must demonstrate that you meet the following language test score in English or French.</p><p><strong>Table 3</strong>. Language requirements.</p><div class="goa-table"><table><thead><tr><th>National Occupational Classification (NOC) TEER category</th><th>Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) test score required</th><th>Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens (NCLC) test score required</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>If your job offer is for NOC 0, 1, 2 or 3 occupation</td><td>Minimum of 5 for each English language skill</td><td>Minimum of 5 for each French language skill</td></tr><tr><td>If your job offer is for NOC 4 or 5 occupation</td><td>Minimum of 4 for each English language skill</td><td>Minimum of 4 for each French language skill</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>You must provide your official test results with your AAIP application for one of the following language tests:</p><ul><li><a href="https://www.celpip.ca/" target="_blank">Canadian English Language Proficiency Index Program (CELPIP) General Test</a></li><li><a href="http://www.ielts.org/test_takers_information.aspx" target="_blank">International English Language Testing System (IELTS) General Training Test</a></li><li><a href="https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-core" target="_blank">Pearson Test of English (PTE)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/en/tests-diplomas/test-for-evaluating-french-tef/tef-canada/" target="_blank">Test d'évaluation de français Canada (TEF Canada)</a></li><li><a href="https://www.france-education-international.fr/test/tcf-canada" target="_blank">Test de connaissance du français Canada (TCF Canada)</a></li></ul><p>Official test results must be less than 2 years old at the time your application was submitted. AAIP will not accept confirmation of registration to take a language test in place of a test result.</p><p>Check the <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/operational-bulletins-manuals/standard-requirements/language-requirements/test-equivalency-charts.html" target="_blank">IRCC guideline on CLB NCLC equivalency</a> for each of the tests.</p>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Education requirements</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted, you must have completed a minimum of high school education equivalent to the Canadian education standard.</p><h4>Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)</h4><p>At the time you submit your application, you must provide a copy of an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) issued by an <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/immigrant-serving-organizations/best-practices/foreign-educational-credential-assessment.html" target="_blank">organization designated by IRCC</a> for your highest level of education. If your ECA report shows that your credential is not equal to completion of at least a Canadian high school diploma, or the ECA states your foreign educational institution is not recognized, you do not meet the education requirement.</p><p>You do not need to provide an ECA if:</p><ul><li>you have a Canadian degree, diploma or certificate from a Canadian post-secondary, technical college or secondary institution listed as:</li><li>a recognized institution in the Directory of Educational Institutions in Canada, or</li><li>an authorized, registered, or licensed institution in the Directory of Educational Institutions in Canada, and the institution was authorized by Advanced Education (for Alberta credentials) or a Canadian provincial or territorial body responsible for education (for credentials issued elsewhere in Canada) to grant your degree, diploma or certificate. Contact your educational institution, Advanced Education (Alberta credentials only) or the appropriate provincial or territorial ministry to confirm if your institution had the authority to grant your credential.</li><li>you have a valid Alberta Qualification Certificate or a trade certificate recognized by Alberta Apprenticeship and Industry Training (AIT).</li></ul><p>AAIP reserves the right to request an ECA from any candidate or to seek an internal educational assessment from the International Qualification Assessment Service (IQAS) or Advanced Education to confirm a candidate meets the minimum Canadian high school equivalent requirement.</p>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Settlement fund requirements</h3><p>At the time your application is submitted and at the time <abbr title="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program">AAIP</abbr> assesses your application, you must demonstrate that you have sufficient funds to support yourself and your family members to settle in your designated community if:</p><ul><li>you are not residing in Canada, or</li><li>you are currently in Canada but are not working</li></ul><p>Minimum settlement funds requirements are based on the Low Income Cut-Offs (LICOs). The amount of settlement funds you must have depends on the number of family members you have, including yourself, and the population size of your designated community where you plan to settle in Alberta.</p><p><strong>Table 4</strong>. Settlement funds requirement for Rural Renewal Stream in Canadian dollars.</p><div class="goa-table"><table><thead><tr><th>&nbsp;</th><th colspan="3">Population size of the community and funds required</th></tr><tr><th>Number of family members</th><th>Less than 1,000</th><th>1,000 to 30,000</th><th>30,000 to 99,999</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1</td><td class="goa-table-number">$8,922</td><td class="goa-table-number">$10,151</td><td class="goa-table-number">$11,093</td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td class="goa-table-number">$11,107</td><td class="goa-table-number">$12,636</td><td class="goa-table-number">$13,810</td></tr><tr><td>3</td><td class="goa-table-number">$13,655</td><td class="goa-table-number">$15,534</td><td class="goa-table-number">$16,977</td></tr><tr><td>4</td><td class="goa-table-number">$16,579</td><td class="goa-table-number">$18,861</td><td class="goa-table-number">$20,613</td></tr><tr><td>5</td><td class="goa-table-number">$18,803</td><td class="goa-table-number">$21,392</td><td class="goa-table-number">$23,379</td></tr><tr><td>6</td><td class="goa-table-number">$21,208</td><td class="goa-table-number">$24,127</td><td class="goa-table-number">$26,367</td></tr><tr><td>7</td><td class="goa-table-number">$23,611</td><td class="goa-table-number">$26,861</td><td class="goa-table-number">$29,356</td></tr><tr><td>Amount of funds required for&nbsp;<br>each additional family member</td><td class="goa-table-number">$2,404</td><td class="goa-table-number">$2,735</td><td class="goa-table-number">$2,989</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>Funds must be unencumbered and readily available to you to meet the minimum settlement funds requirement. Real estate property, jewelry, cars and other personal assets are not eligible.</p><p>If your spouse is coming with you, you can count money you have together in a joint account. You may be able to count money in an account under your spouse’s name only, but you must prove you have access to the money.</p><p>The funds must be available at the time of application and assessment. You must prove to an AAIP officer that you can legally access the funds when you arrive in Alberta.</p><p>You must provide official letters from any banks or financial institutions where your funds are kept.</p><p>Letters must:</p><ul><li>be printed on the financial institution’s letterhead</li><li>include contact information of the financial institution – address, telephone number and email address</li><li>include your name</li><li>list all outstanding debts such as credit card debts and loans</li><li>include, for each current bank and investment account, the:<ul><li>account numbers</li><li>date each account was opened</li><li>current balance of each account</li></ul></li></ul><p>In addition, ensure you include bank statements showing transactions made within the past 3 months, and the average balance for the past 6 months.</p>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <div class="goa-callout"><p>Ensure you review each section to confirm all requirements have been met. Ensure all necessary documentation is submitted for a complete application.</p></div>

  </div>
