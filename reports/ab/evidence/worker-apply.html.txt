  <h2>Overview</h2><p>You can only apply to an Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) worker stream if you are invited to do so. You must complete a Worker Expression of Interest (WEOI) to be invited.</p><p>Follow the steps below to complete your <abbr title="Expression of Interest">EOI</abbr> and potentially be invited to submit an application. The worker streams and their pathways are:</p><ul><li>Alberta Opportunity Stream</li><li>Alberta Express Entry Stream<ul><li>Dedicated Health Care Pathways (Express Entry and Non-Express Entry options)</li><li>Law Enforcement Pathway</li><li>Accelerated Tech Pathway</li></ul></li><li>Rural Renewal Stream</li><li>Tourism and Hospitality Stream</li></ul><p>Read <a href="/system/files/jeti-aaip-helpful-hints.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="b6063d36-db6c-4751-b24e-ba9331801789" data-entity-substitution="media" title="jeti-aaip-helpful-hints.pdf">Helpful Hints for the AAIP Portal</a> for tips on using the <abbr title="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program">AAIP</abbr> portal to complete, update and monitor the status of AAIP Worker EOI and AAIP application or to report technical issues with the portal.</p>

  <li id="accordion53067">
  <h3 class="goa-title">Step 1. Submit a Worker Expression of Interest</h3>
  <div class="goa-text">
    
  <p>You must submit a Worker Expression of Interest (WEOI) to be considered for an invitation to apply to one of AAIP's worker streams. Effective April 7, 2026, a $135 fee applies.</p><p>In the WEOI, you will self-declare information. Once submitted, you will be entered into the Worker Stream pool, where your WEOI will be scored and ranked using a points grid.</p><p>Your WEOI will remain valid in the pool for 12 months. During that time, AAIP conducts regular draws, selecting candidates based on WEOI scores, provincial labour market needs and priorities, available nominations, and application volumes. Selected candidates will receive an invitation to apply by email.</p><p>If your WEOI is not selected within 12 months, it will expire. To remain in the pool, you must submit a new WEOI and pay the applicable fee.</p><h4>A. Terms and conditions</h4><p>AAIP has sole discretion in selecting candidates to apply to the program. AAIP does not guarantee a Worker EOI will be assessed, processed, or otherwise actioned, or that an individual who meets the selection draw parameters will be invited to apply.</p><p>While you will indicate your preferred stream in your Worker EOI, there is no guarantee you will be invited to apply to your preferred stream or at all, and AAIP may choose to invite you to apply to a different stream. AAIP may also cancel Worker EOIs in the pool to support technology updates or changes to the Worker EOI or the Worker EOI points grid.</p><p>You are responsible for the information provided to AAIP in your Worker EOI and any subsequent application. When AAIP selects candidates, it expects that information contained in the Worker EOI is accurate. If you are invited to submit an AAIP application, AAIP will validate the information you declared in the Worker EOI was accurate at the time it was submitted. Incorrect or fraudulent information could result in the AAIP application being declined, and you could be banned from applying to AAIP for a period of time.</p><h4>B. Prepare to submit your Worker EOI</h4><p>Important considerations before you start your WEOI:</p><ul><li>You can only have one WEOI.</li><li>You cannot create a WEOI if you have an existing WEOI, Entrepreneur EOI, or a draft or active AAIP application.<ul><li><strong>Note:</strong> candidates with an active application who meet the requirements for their stream are not encouraged to withdraw their application to be considered for another stream. Selection is not guaranteed, and a new fee and full processing times would apply.<ul><li>To withdraw an application or business application, submit a request through the ‘Create New Request’ section of the AAIP portal. AAIP will review your request and notify you by email once complete.</li><li>Application fees for the withdrawn application are non-refundable.</li></ul></li><li>To cancel a Worker or Entrepreneur EOI, access your submitted EOI in the <a href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">AAIP portal</a>, scroll to the bottom, and select “Cancel”.<ul><li>Fees for cancelled Worker or Entrepreneur EOIs are non-refundable.</li><li>Cancelled EOIs cannot be reinstated.</li></ul></li></ul></li><li>Confirm you meet the eligibility requirements for at least one AAIP worker stream before submitting an EOI:<ul><li><a href="/aaip-alberta-opportunity-stream-eligibility" target="_blank" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="8211c396-b516-4300-877b-7407f0e8ca09" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Alberta Opportunity Stream – Eligibility">Alberta Opportunity Stream eligibility requirements</a></li><li><a href="/aaip-alberta-express-entry-stream-eligibility" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="f69ca564-d9ee-4fb4-9b9a-c6951489fcba" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Alberta Express Entry Stream – Eligibility">Alberta Express Entry Stream eligibility requirements</a><ul><li><a href="/dedicated-health-care-pathway" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="758e08d7-f011-405f-8dca-a3c126090bb5" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Dedicated Health Care Pathway">Dedicated Health Care Pathway eligibility requirements</a></li></ul></li><li><a href="/tourism-and-hospitality-stream-eligibility" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="cdedc4e0-7f61-4edd-b6e6-b8f3f2c07dda" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Tourism and Hospitality Stream – Eligibility">Tourism and Hospitality Stream eligibility requirements</a></li><li><a href="/aaip-rural-renewal-stream-eligibility" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="ef07886e-e963-4f2b-8189-3e5a0fc7cb99" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Rural Renewal Stream – Eligibility">Rural Renewal Stream eligibility requirements</a><ul><li>Note: To qualify for selection and receive WEOI points, an endorsement letter from a designated community is required at the time of WEOI submission.</li></ul></li></ul></li><li>Make sure you have a valid Alberta.ca Account. You may use an existing basic or verified Alberta.ca Account. &nbsp;<ul><li>An Alberta.ca account is a free online identity for accessing Government of Alberta services online.<ul><li><a href="https://account.alberta.ca/ui/sign-in/signin">Learn how to create an Alberta.ca account</a></li></ul></li><li>AAIP does not recommend that you share your Alberta.ca Account with others, including family members and third-party representatives, as your Worker EOI and any subsequent application could be modified without your knowledge or consent.</li></ul></li><li>Be ready to complete the entire WEOI.<ul><li>You cannot save a draft of the WEOI.</li><li>Review the WEOI points grid to understand the information you must provide, how points are awarded, and minimum language proficiency requirements.</li></ul></li></ul><h4>C. Review the WEOI points grid</h4><p>AAIP will award points based on a points grid:</p><ul><li>The maximum points that may be obtained is 100.</li><li>You do not need to meet a minimum point score to submit a Worker EOI.</li><li><a href="/system/files/im-worker-stream-expression-of-interest-points-grid.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="d844fa72-a9c7-4845-9051-5da5d11623ce" data-entity-substitution="media" title="im-worker-stream-expression-of-interest-points-grid.pdf">Download the Worker Stream Expression of Interest points grid</a>.</li></ul><p>AAIP also collects the following information in the WEOI to support draw selections that align with sector labour needs and other program priorities. No points are awarded for these factors:</p><ul><li>Status with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (work permit status and federal Express Entry profile information)</li><li><a href="https://noc.esdc.gc.ca/" target="_blank">National Occupational Classification (NOC) code (2021 version)</a> of your Alberta Job offer occupation</li><li>Primary line of business of your Alberta job offer employer identified using a <a href="https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p3VD.pl?Function=getVD&amp;TVD=1369825" target="_blank">4-digit North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) code (2022 version)</a></li><li>Wage and hours of work</li></ul><h4>D. Keep your WEOI updated</h4><p>You can monitor your WEOI status in the Check Existing WEOI section of the <a href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">AAIP portal</a>.</p><p>Your WEOI must contain true and accurate information at all times.</p><p><strong>Updating your WEOI</strong></p><ul><li>You can update your WEOI using the ‘Edit’ function in the AAIP portal.<ul><li>To make changes:<ul><li>Log in to the AAIP portal, navigate to your WEOI, and select Edit at the bottom of the page.</li><li>Your WEOI must be in ‘Submitted’ status to be edited.</li></ul></li><li>While editing:<ul><li>The status will change to ‘Edit Submission’.</li><li>Your WEOI will not be eligible for selection while it is being edited.</li></ul></li><li>After editing:<ul><li>Once you save your changes, your WEOI will return to ‘Submitted’ status, and your WEOI will re‑enter the pool.</li><li>If no changes are saved within 1 hour, the WEOI will automatically switch back to ‘Submitted’ status with no updates applied.</li></ul></li></ul></li></ul><p><strong>WEOI validity period</strong></p><ul><li>WEOIs are valid for one year after submission. Editing your does not change your WEOI validity.<ul><li>For WEOI’s created on or after April 7, 2026, validity is one year from the date the WEOI fee was paid.</li><li>For WEOI’s created April 6, 2026, or earlier, validity is one year from the date the WEOI was created.</li></ul></li><li>If your WEOI is not selected within 12 months, it will be cancelled. To re-enter the selection pool, you must submit a new WEOI and pay the applicable fee.</li></ul><p>Review information on selection draws on the <a href="/aaip-processing-information" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="144a70e2-e9c0-4976-918e-40e7244fdc70" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program – Processing information">AAIP processing information</a> page.</p>

  <h3 class="goa-title">Step 2. Receive an invitation to apply to a specific AAIP worker stream</h3>
  <div class="goa-text">
    
  <p>AAIP conducts ongoing selection draws from the WEOI pool. If you are selected, you will receive an email from AAIP inviting you to submit an application for a specific stream or pathway. The email will be sent to the Alberta.ca Account email address used to create your WEOI.</p><p>Your status in the AAIP portal will state Selected. Before accepting your invitation:</p><ul><li>Review the eligibility criteria for the stream you have been invited to apply to</li><li>Review the&nbsp;<a href="/system/files/jeti-aaip-worker-streams-document-checklist.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="ce478fe7-036e-4f59-81ec-94d92604e066" data-entity-substitution="media" title="jeti-aaip-worker-streams-document-checklist.pdf">Worker Stream Document Checklist</a> to confirm you have or can secure all required documents.</li></ul><p>If you do not meet eligibility requirements or cannot provide all required documents, do not proceed with your application. Applications that do not meet AAIP requirements will be declined. The WEOI fee and application fees will not be refunded, and a new WEOI and fee would be required to re-enter the selection pool.</p><p><strong>Invitation timelines and options</strong></p><ul><li>You have 15 days to accept this invitation.</li><li>If you do not want to accept the invitation, you can use your unique link to select “Decline Invitation” before the 15 day invitation period ends. Declining the invitation will return your WEOI to the pool.</li><li>If you intend to apply, you must click on the invitation link and create and save a draft application before the invitation expires.&nbsp;</li><li>If you do not select ‘Create Application’ within 15 days, your WEOI will automatically return to the &nbsp;pool with a status of Submitted.</li></ul><p><strong>Creating an application</strong></p><ul><li>When you select the invitation link, you will be directed to the AAIP portal, where a ‘Create Application’ button will appear.</li><li>Clicking ‘Create Application’ and saving the first page creates a draft application.</li><li>If you decide not to proceed after creating a draft application and want to re‑enter the selection pool, you must:<ul><li>cancel the draft application, and</li><li>cancel your existing WEOI, then</li><li>submit a new WEOI and pay a new WEOI fee.</li></ul></li></ul>

Follow the instructions to apply</h4><p>You have 15 days to click on the link to start the application. Understand AAIP application terms and conditions</h4><p>The availability of AAIP streams, categories, pathways and pilots is dependent upon application volumes and labour market needs. Changes to AAIP, including notice of suspension or closure of its streams, categories, pathways and pilots and criteria changes, are available at&nbsp;<a href="/aaip-updates" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="bec57694-6b9e-4a34-8d50-d8a86c1d79ae" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program – Updates">AAIP Updates</a>.</p><p>AAIP is not obligated to assess/process any applications submitted. The Alberta government does not use email accounts such as Gmail, Hotmail, Yahoo, etc. If you receive a suspicious email, contact your local police and report the incident to AAIP using the <a href="/contact-opportunity-alberta" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="b3c24993-6b17-439e-aead-25c29a1da184" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Contact Opportunity Alberta">Opportunity Alberta contact form</a>.</p>

  <li id="accordion53074">
  <h3 class="goa-title">Step 5. Receive a decision on your AAIP application</h3>
  <div class="goa-text">
    
  <p>You will receive a decision on your application by email. If you identified an immigration representative to act on your behalf for your application, the decision email will be sent to you and copied to your representative.</p><h4>A. Approved applications</h4><p>If your application is approved, you will become a Provincial Nominee. Log in to the <a href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">AAIP portal</a> to view your nomination certificate. Make sure you review the information in the nomination certificate carefully.</p><p>Review the AAIP’s ’After you are nominated page’ for your stream for next steps.</p><p>Review the AAIP’s After you are nominated page for your stream for next steps.</p><h4>B. Declined applications</h4><p>If your AAIP application is declined, log in to the <a href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">AAIP portal</a> to review the reasons for decline.</p><p>If you can demonstrate an error was made in assessing your application you may be eligible to submit a request for reconsideration.</p><h4>C. Request for reconsideration</h4><p>If you receive a decision from the AAIP and believe a mistake was made, you may submit a request for reconsideration if you meet the program requirements.</p><p>You may submit only one request for reconsideration for each application.</p><p>Your request must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the date on your decision letter and must explain how the decision may have been made in error.</p><p><strong>Who can submit a request</strong></p><p>A request for reconsideration must:</p><ul><li>relate directly to the reasons in the decision letter</li><li>show that a mistake was made in applying program criteria to the application</li><li>be submitted by the applicant named in the decision letter or their authorized representative</li><li>be submitted within 30 calendar days of the date the decision letter was issued</li></ul><p><strong>What a request for reconsideration cannot be used for</strong></p><p>A request for reconsideration cannot be used to:</p><ul><li>submit new information or documents</li><li>re-state information or evidence that was already submitted with the application</li><li>ask for an exemption from program requirements</li><li>repeat information that has already been provided</li><li>challenge a decision only because the applicant disagrees with it</li><li>request humanitarian and compassionate consideration</li></ul><p><strong>What information will be reviewed</strong></p><p>A request for reconsideration is based only on the information that was on file when the original decision was made.</p><p><strong>New information or documents will not be considered.</strong> This includes information or documents that were not submitted before the decision was issued.</p><p>During assessment, applicants may be given an opportunity to respond if concerns are identified. Applicants must provide all relevant information and documentation within the timelines provided. Failure to do so may result in a decision being made based on the information already on file.</p><p>Applicants are responsible for submitting all required information and supporting documents during the application assessment process, including in response to a procedural fairness request. Where a procedural fairness request is issued, it is the final opportunity to provide information or documents in support of an application. Information or documents submitted after that stage will not be considered.</p><p><strong>After a decision is made</strong></p><p>Once a decision has been issued, the application record is considered complete for the purpose of reconsideration.</p><p>The AAIP does not accept or consider information or documents submitted outside of a requested stage of processing. Documents must only be submitted when requested by the program.</p>


      </div>
</li>




		</ul>
	</div>
