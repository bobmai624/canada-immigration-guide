  <h2>Before you apply</h2><p>You may only have one Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) application in process at any time.</p><h3>How to withdraw an application in process</h3><p>If you choose to request the withdrawal of an application, you must submit a request to <abbr title="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program">AAIP</abbr>. AAIP must confirm that your application has been withdrawn before a new application can be submitted in the AAIP portal.</p><p>The Business Application fee for the withdrawn Business Application will not be refunded.</p><div class="goa-callout"><p>Candidates must submit their request to withdraw a Business Application using the AAIP portal.</p></div><h4>Complete the online request</h4><ul><li>Log into the <a href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">Alberta Advantage Immigration Program</a> portal</li><li>On the AAIP portal main page, click on 'Create New Request'.</li><li>Find the AAIP Business Application number for the Business Application that you want to withdraw.</li><li>Once you click on the Business Application number, you will be directed to a page with detailed information. You can only see request options that are available to you for this Business Application. If you are eligible to withdraw the Business Application, a blue Withdraw Application button will be available above the Business Application number.</li><li>Click on the blue 'Withdraw Application' button.</li><li>Next, read the <a href="https://open.alberta.ca/publications/p28p5" target="_blank"><em>Protection of Privacy Act</em></a> (POPA) clause and other important information about completing a request. Check off the box and click on 'Continue'. A draft is automatically saved once you click on the 'Continue' button. If you experience any technical issues or leave the request section while creating the request, you will need to go to the 'Check Existing Requests' page to retrieve the draft request to continue.</li><li>Read the information provided on submitting a withdrawal. Once a Business Application is withdrawn it cannot be re-activated. Enter your reason for requesting the withdrawal of the Business Application, and click on 'Submit Request' button.</li></ul><p>AAIP will review your request and notify you by email after the review is completed.</p>

  <h2>How to apply</h2><div class="goa-callout"><p>There are 7 steps in the Rural Entrepreneur Stream application process. Read each step carefully before you apply.</p></div><h3>Step 1. Read the AAIP terms and conditions</h3><p>The availability of <abbr title="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program">AAIP</abbr> streams, categories, pathways and pilots is dependent upon application volumes and labour market needs. AAIP reserves the right to close application intake for any AAIP stream, category, pathway or pilot at any time, without prior notice. Further, AAIP may decline to consider applications to closed or suspended streams, categories, pathways or pilots, regardless of when the applications were submitted. The criteria for AAIP are routinely evaluated and may be subject to change at any time and without notice. Changes to AAIP, including notice of suspension or closure of its streams, categories, pathways and pilots and criteria changes, are available at <a href="/aaip-updates">AAIP – Updates</a>.</p><p>AAIP is not obligated to assess/process any applications submitted. Applications to AAIP are treated as an expression of interest, and as such, will be processed according to quality of the application (completeness, eligibility), labour market information, occupational supply and demand forecasting, AAIP application volumes or any other factors at AAIP’s discretion. By applying to AAIP you acknowledge and agree that your application may not be processed in the order received, or at all. Further, the decision to assess or process any particular application, and the outcome of any such assessment / processing, is at AAIP’s sole discretion.</p><p>Program criteria are eligibility minimums, and meeting program criteria does not guarantee that a candidate’s application will be assessed, processed, or granted. AAIP may choose to assess applications with the most current criteria irrespective of the date of submission of an application.</p><p>Candidates must have a strong likelihood of becoming economically established in Alberta and AAIP reserves the right to decline applications if AAIP is of the opinion that the candidate may not economically establish in Alberta.</p><h3>Step 2. Review the Eligibility Criteria</h3><p>Review the <a href="/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-eligibility">Rural Entrepreneur Stream Eligibility Criteria</a> and the AAIP Terms and Conditions (see Step 1 above) before submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI).</p><p>AAIP does not guarantee that all complete Business Applications received will be assessed, or that all candidates who meet the eligibility criteria will be issued a Business Application Approval Letter and/or nomination. Review the AAIP Terms and Conditions for complete information (see Step 1 above). Additionally, review the economic establishment information on the <a href="/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-eligibility" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="1730d83d-3ed4-44ad-9027-19935d54b55f" data-entity-substitution="canonical" title="Rural Entrepreneur Stream – Eligibility">Rural Entrepreneur Stream Eligibility</a> page to understand the factors AAIP may consider when assessing this requirement.</p><p>Submission of a Business Application or nomination by AAIP does not guarantee you will be issued a permanent resident visa.</p><h3>Step 3. Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI)</h3><p>If you meet the Rural Entrepreneur Stream criteria, you can submit your Expression of Interest (EOI) by accessing the AAIP Portal. AAIP will review and score your EOI submission. Highest-ranking candidates will be requested to submit a Business Application.</p><p>For more information on the criteria used to assess EOIs, see the&nbsp;<a href="/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-eligibility">Rural Entrepreneur Stream Eligibility</a> page.</p><p>The Rural Entrepreneur Stream Business Application form is not available online. Only candidates who receive a request to submit a Business Application will be able to access the AAIP portal to start the Business Application process.</p><h4>Using the AAIP online portal</h4><p>AAIP uses an online system to submit EOIs and Business Applications. You will need:</p><ul><li>valid email address</li><li>access to a computer</li><li>ability to scan and upload the required documents to the AAIP portal</li></ul><p><a class="goa-cta" href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">AAIP online portal</a></p><p>Before entering your application information in the portal, read the <a href="/system/files/jeti-aaip-helpful-hints.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="b6063d36-db6c-4751-b24e-ba9331801789" data-entity-substitution="media" title="jeti-aaip-helpful-hints.pdf">AAIP Portal Helpful Hints</a> for tips on filling out the online application.</p><p>The AAIP portal is for candidates only. You are responsible for your AAIP online portal account. Portal access is not available for third party representatives and family members.</p><p>You must have a basic <a href="https://account.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">Alberta.ca Account</a>&nbsp;to access the portal. An Alberta.ca Account is a free online identity for accessing Government of Alberta services online. Instructions on how to create an Alberta.ca Account are in the portal and on the&nbsp;Alberta.ca Account website. You may also use an existing basic or verified Alberta.ca Account.</p><p>The email address used to set up your Alberta.ca Account is the email address that AAIP will use to correspond with you. If you need to change your email address for corresponding with AAIP, you must update your Alberta.ca Account.</p><p>You should not share your Alberta.ca Account with others, including family members and third-party representatives, as your information or documents could be modified without your knowledge or consent.</p><p>You are responsible for the information provided to AAIP for your EOI and business application. Incorrect or fraudulent information could result in your business application being declined, and you could be banned from applying to AAIP for a period of time.</p><h4>Submitting an EOI</h4><p>For your EOI to be accepted for processing by AAIP, you must complete 2 steps:</p><ul><li>complete and submit the online EOI, and</li><li>pay the EOI fee</li></ul><h4>Complete and submit your EOI</h4><p>Entrepreneurs can submit an EOI for the Rural Entrepreneur Stream through the AAIP portal. See the <a href="/system/files/im-aaip-business-plan-guidelines.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="dcd86d96-68c7-4da4-8c10-4282daa4ea15" data-entity-substitution="media" title="im-aaip-business-plan-guidelines.pdf">AAIP Business Plan (Guidelines)</a> for what to include in your business plan when submitting your EOI to AAIP.</p><p>Once you start your EOI in the AAIP portal, you have 120 days to complete and submit the required documents. After 120 days, your draft EOI will be purged. You can submit a new EOI if you choose.</p><p>You must ensure your EOI is up to date at the time of submission, and that all your information is complete, true, and correct.</p><p>Once your EOI is completed, you can submit it to AAIP by clicking the Submit button in the AAIP portal. A file number will be provided to you in the portal and will also be listed on the MyEOI page.</p><p>You must pay the EOI fee before your EOI is accepted for processing by AAIP.</p><h4>Pay the fee</h4><p>There is a fee for submitting an EOI. A fee of $200 applies to all EOIs. Each submitted EOI requires a separate application fee.</p><h4>Fee conditions</h4><p>The AAIP EOI fee covers the cost of processing your EOI and is non-refundable once an EOI has been submitted to AAIP, regardless of outcome. There is no guarantee that you will be invited to submit a business application based on your EOI. Refund requests will not be considered for any reason.</p><h4>How to pay your fee</h4><p>Instructions on how to pay your fee are available in the portal. For any online fee payment issues, contact <a href="https://eservices.alberta.ca/contact/">eServices</a>.</p><p>You will need to pay your fee within 24 hours of submitting your complete application in the portal. The $200 fee must be paid online using a single credit or debit card.</p><p>If you do not pay your fee within 24 hours of submitting your EOI, your EOI will be cancelled. Cancelled EOIs cannot be reinstated and a new EOI must be created and submitted.</p><p>Check the EOI status on the MyEOI page to confirm if your EOI fee has been paid.</p><ul><li>If the EOI Status is 'Payment Pending' on the MyEOI page, your EOI has not been accepted for processing by AAIP and your fee has not been paid.</li><li>If the EOI status on the MyEOI page is 'Pending Review by the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program', your EOI has been accepted for processing by AAIP. You will receive an email with your fee payment receipt.</li></ul><p>You must ensure that your EOI has the status of ‘'Pending Review by the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program’ or it will not be reviewed by AAIP.</p><p><strong>Note</strong>: Once your EOI is submitted, you will not be able to update it until AAIP has reviewed and scored it, and the status is ‘Eligible.’ If your EOI status is ‘Not Eligible’ then you will not be able to update it and must submit a new EOI if you choose. AAIP will score your EOI submission.</p><h4>EOI pool</h4><p>EOIs are placed in the pool of qualified candidates for a period of 12 months from the date of their original submission. If your EOI is not selected in this 12-month period, it will be cancelled, and you will have to submit a new EOI if you choose.</p><p>While your EOI is in the pool of qualified candidates, you can update or cancel your submission by accessing the AAIP portal. By updating or cancelling your submission, you will no longer be in the pool of qualified candidates (if you choose to resubmit and pay the processing fee). Your new EOI (if you choose to resubmit and pay the processing fee) will have a new submission date and it will be assessed against the program criteria at that time.</p><p>Candidate’s information will be assessed based on the Rural Entrepreneur Stream Points Grid. Candidates with the highest-ranking points will be contacted directly by AAIP&nbsp;and invited to submit a Business Application. Only candidates who receive a request to submit a Business Application under the Rural Entrepreneur Stream will be considered.</p><h4>Important</h4><p>An EOI is not an application. There is no guarantee that if you meet the selection requirements you will receive a request to submit a Business Application. Requests to submit a Business Application are affected by the volume of potential candidates meeting these requirements, the type (occupation or sector) of submissions in AAIP processing queues, and the number of available nominations.</p>

AAIP will update its internal system with changes.</p><h4>Forms to notify AAIP of updates</h4><ul><li>A signed <a href="/system/files/custom_downloaded_images/lbr-aaip-candidate-representative-form.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="6a1d5ebf-65be-44a2-b5a1-b0febd1e1def" data-entity-substitution="media" title="lbr-aaip-candidate-representative-form.pdf">Use of Representative form</a> must be provided to change your third-party representative for your AAIP application</li></ul><h4>To withdraw a Business Application or to update your contact information (excluding email address):</h4><ul><li>Log into the&nbsp;<a href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">Alberta Advantage Immigration Program portal</a></li><li>On the AAIP portal main page, click on ‘Create New Request’.</li><li>Find the AAIP Business Application number for the Business Application for which you want to make a request.</li><li>Once you click on the Business Application number, you will be directed to a page with detailed information. You can only see request options that are available to you for this Business Application.<ul><li>Withdrawal: If you are eligible to withdraw the Business Application, a blue Withdraw Application button will be available above the Business Application number.</li><li>Update Contact Information: You can update contact information for all active or nominated Business Applications. Update family information: You can update spouse/common-law partner and dependent information for all active and nominated Business Applications.</li><li>Request change of representative: You can update your representative for all Business Applications, including withdrawn and declined Business Applications.</li><li>Click on the blue button for the type of request you want to submit.</li></ul></li><li>Next, read the <a href="https://open.alberta.ca/publications/p28p5" target="_blank">POPA clause</a> and other important information about completing a request. Check off the box and click on ‘Continue’. A draft is automatically saved once you click on the ‘Continue’ button. If you experience any technical issues or leave the request section while creating the request, you will need to go to the ‘Check Existing Requests’ page to retrieve the draft request to continue.</li><li>Read the information provided on submitting the request and follow the instructions to complete the request.<ul><li>Withdrawal: Once a Business Application is withdrawn, it cannot be re-activated. Enter your reason for requesting the withdrawal of the Business Application.</li><li>Update contact information: The current contact information AAIP has will be displayed. Only update the information that is no longer correct. Do not delete any existing information that is correct. Include only your contact information. Do not provide anyone else’s information, including that of your third party representative.</li><li>Update family information: Provide details on the spouse/common-law partner and/ or dependent you need to add or remove from the Business Application and upload supporting documents.</li><li>Request change of representative: If you are removing a representative and representing yourself, fill out the fields as directed in the portal. If you are appointing a representative or changing from one representative to another representative, read the provided AAIP Use of Representative policy and upload a signed <a href="/system/files/custom_downloaded_images/lbr-aaip-candidate-representative-form.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="978cf9fb-46ec-4dec-b0a7-59e83af44e81" data-entity-substitution="media" title="lbr-aaip-candidate-representative-form.pdf">Use of Representative form</a>. Read the instructions in the form to determine which pages must be completed for the change to representative you are requesting.</li></ul></li><li>Click on ‘Submit Request’ button.</li></ul><p>AAIP will review your request to withdraw and will notify you by email after the review is completed.</p><p>We will make the changes to your contact information in our internal system. Contact information changes will only be reflected in the Update Contact Information section in the portal. Changes to your contact information will not be reflected in the online Business Application.</p><h4>If more information is needed</h4><p>A program officer with AAIP will contact you by email or phone if more information is needed to assess your Business Application. You must submit this information in the format requested by the program officer to their government email address. The only other information you should send to your assigned program officer is documents showing changes to your contact information, work permit status or family situation.</p>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Step 5. Business Application assessment</h3><p>Once the Business Application and supporting documents are received, AAIP will assess your Business Application. There is no guarantee that all complete Business Applications will be assessed or that all candidates who meet the criteria will be issued a nomination.</p><p>An AAIP program officer may contact you by email or phone if additional information is needed to assess your Business Application and will tell you how to send the information.</p><p>Your Business Application will be assessed against the <a href="/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-eligibility">eligibility criteria</a> and your EOI Points will be verified. Find more information on the criteria used to assess EOIs and Business Applications.</p><p>AAIP may contact you for more information. If you do not respond to requests for additional information, your Business Application will be declined.</p><p><strong>If your Business Application is approved</strong></p><p>If AAIP deems that you have demonstrated that you meet all the Rural Entrepreneur Stream criteria, you will be sent a Business Performance Agreement by email for your review and signature.</p><p>Your Business Performance Agreement is a legal agreement between you and the Province of Alberta. Your Business Performance Agreement will include:</p><ul><li>the industry sector your business will operate within</li><li>the term length of the agreement</li><li>a list of other information relevant to your Business Application, including but not limited to:<ul><li>job creation</li><li>business succession</li><li>business location</li><li>business investment</li></ul></li></ul><p>The Business Performance Agreement requirements are based upon the information you provided in your Business Application and business plan.</p><p>AAIP does not set restrictions on the hiring of temporary residents, Canadian citizens or permanent residents for your business. However, in order to maintain eligibility under the Rural Entrepreneur Stream and be considered for nomination, candidates must demonstrate they meet the job creation criteria identified in the Business Performance Agreement for Canadian citizens, or permanent residents, and in accordance with all terms and conditions of the Business Performance Agreement.</p><p>You must sign, scan, and email your Business Performance Agreement to AAIP within 14 days.</p><p><strong>Important</strong>: AAIP will not consider any requests to change the terms of the Business Performance Agreement other than an extension to the term of your Business Performance Agreement after it is approved and signed by you.</p><p>Once all parties have signed the Business Performance Agreement, AAIP will provide you with a copy of the signed Business Performance Agreement, a Business Application Approval Letter along with a 205(a) Letter of Support. The 205(a) Letter of Support allows you to apply for a two-year work permit to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). There is no cost associated with this 205(a) Letter of Support. Any subsequent 205(a) Letter of Support request will require a $150 processing fee.</p><p>Entrepreneurs who are approved under the <a href="/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-eligibility#established-businesses-eligibility-criteria">established businesses in Alberta criteria</a> are not required to sign a Business Performance Agreement. These entrepreneurs will be issued a nomination certificate to apply for permanent residence to IRCC.</p><h4>Applying for a work permit</h4><p>A work permit is the official document issued by the Government of Canada that allows a foreign national to work legally on a temporary basis in Canada. The duration of the work permit validity period may vary at the discretion of IRCC.</p><p>You are required to submit your application for a work permit to IRCC along with your 205(a) Letter of Support within 3 months of receiving this letter, to facilitate your arrival in Alberta within 12 months of receiving your Business Application Approval Letter.</p><p>To apply for a work permit, follow the instructions on <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/permit/temporary/apply.html" target="_blank">IRCC’s website</a>.</p><p>You must first register as an employer in <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/partners-service-providers/employer-portal.html" target="_blank">IRCC’s Employer Portal</a> as the employer of your business (self-employed person). You will need to pay an employer compliance fee but you will be exempt from the LMIA fee.</p><p>Issuance of a 205(a) Letter of Support does not guarantee that you will receive a work permit. Only IRCC can issue a work permit.</p><p>After receiving the work permit, you can move to Alberta to establish your business. Review the information on:</p><ul><li><a href="/make-alberta-your-home">preparing to come to Alberta</a></li><li><a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/new-immigrants/new-life-canada/pre-arrival-services/prepare-live.html" target="_blank">preparing to live in Canada</a></li></ul><h4>Important</h4><p>The Business Application Approval Letter is <strong>not</strong> a nomination and cannot be used to apply for permanent residence. It recognizes that AAIP approves your Business Application and proposed business outlined in the Business Performance Agreement and expects that you will reside and operate your business in Alberta. The 205(a) Letter of Support will allow you to apply for a work permit to come to Alberta to establish your business.</p><h4>If your Business Application is declined</h4><p>If your Business Application is declined, you will be emailed a decision letter providing the reasons your Business Application was declined. If you identified an immigration representative to act on your behalf for your Business Application, the decision email will be sent to you and copied to your representative.</p><p>If you can demonstrate an error was made in assessing your Business Application, you may be eligible to submit a Request for Reconsideration.</p>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Step 6. Establish your business in Alberta</h3><p>Upon receiving your Business Application Approval Letter and your work permit, you must:</p><ul><li>reside in a rural Alberta community</li><li>actively own and operate an eligible business in a rural Alberta community for at least 12 months with at least 51% ownership if establishing a new business or 100% ownership for business succession</li><li>meet the conditions of the Business Performance Agreement that you signed with the Government of Alberta</li><li>demonstrate that the business is operating successfully by generating product or services after one year of operation</li></ul><p>Once you meet all the conditions of the Business Performance Agreement, submit the Final Report for Nomination as described in Step 7.</p><p>Candidates must:</p><ol><li>Submit an <a href="https://cfr.forms.gov.ab.ca/Form/AINP12706" target="_blank">Arrival Report</a> to AAIP within 30 days of the date that appears as the “date issued” on your work permit issued by the Government of Canada.</li><li>Submit a <a href="https://cfr.forms.gov.ab.ca/Form/AINP12703" target="_blank">Business Progress Report</a> to AAIP after the end of each 6-month period from the date that appears as the “date issued” on your work permit issued by the Government of Canada. If you require more time to meet the conditions of your Business Performance Agreement, you must&nbsp;submit a request to make Business Performance Agreement changes.<ul><li>AAIP may request evidence to confirm you are meeting the conditions of your signed Business Performance Agreement. There is no guarantee that your request for Business Performance Agreement extension will be approved.</li></ul></li></ol><h3>Request for Business Performance Agreement changes:</h3><p><strong>Complete the online request</strong></p><ul><li>Log into the&nbsp;<a href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/" target="_blank">AAIP portal</a></li><li>On the AAIP portal main page, click on 'Create New Request'.</li><li>Find the AAIP Business Application number for the Business Application for which you want to make a request.</li><li>Once you click on the Business Application number, you will be directed to a page with detailed information. You can only see request options that are available to you for this Business Application.<ul><li>Request for Business Performance Agreement Change: If you are eligible to make this request, a blue “Request for Business Performance Agreement Change” button will be available above the Business Application number.</li></ul></li><li>Click on the blue button to submit this request.</li><li>Next, read the <a href="https://open.alberta.ca/publications/p28p5" target="_blank">POPA clause</a> and other important information about completing a request. Check off the box and click on 'Continue'. A draft is automatically saved once you click on the 'Continue' button. If you experience any technical issues or leave the request section while creating the request, you will need to go to the 'Check Existing Requests' page to retrieve the draft request to continue.</li><li>Read the information provided on submitting the request and follow the instructions to complete the request.<ul><li>Enter your reason for requesting the change to Business Performance Agreement and upload any supporting document(s).</li></ul></li><li>Click on 'Submit Request' button.</li><li>Next you must pay the fee.</li></ul><h4>Pay the fee</h4><p>All requests for Business Performance Agreement Changes are subject to a service fee of $150.</p><h4>Fee conditions</h4><p>The request for a Business Performance Agreement Changes fee is non-refundable once a request has been submitted in the AAIP portal. The fee covers the cost of processing your request and is required whether Business Performance Agreement Changes are approved. There is no guarantee AAIP will approve changes to your Business Performance Agreement. Refund requests will not be considered.&nbsp;</p><h4>How to pay your fee</h4><p>Instructions on how to pay your fee are available in the portal. For any online fee payment issues, contact <a href="https://eservices.alberta.ca/contact/">eServices</a>.</p><p>You will need to pay your fee within 24 hours of submitting your request in the portal. The $150 fee must be paid online using a single credit or debit card.</p><p>AAIP will review your request for Business Performance Agreement change and notify you by email after the review is completed.</p><p>If you do not pay your fee within 24 hours of submitting your request, your request will be cancelled. Cancelled requests cannot be reinstated and new request must be created and submitted. Check the Request Status on the Check Existing Requests page to confirm if your request fee has been paid.</p><ul><li>If the status is 'Payment Pending', your fee has not been paid.</li><li>If the status is 'Submitted', your request has been accepted for processing by AAIP. You will receive an email with your fee payment receipt.</li></ul><p>Once your request is submitted, AAIP will review your request for Business Performance Agreement change and notify you by email after the review is completed.</p><h4>Extending your work permit</h4><p>If&nbsp;you need to extend your work permit to meet the conditions of the Business Performance Agreement, you must submit a&nbsp;Request for a 205(a) Letter of Support&nbsp;to AAIP within 90 days of your work permit expiry. All requests for a 205(a) Letter of Support will be reviewed and granted on a case-by-case basis. Requests for a 205(a) letter of Support are subject to a service fee of $150.</p><ul><li>Refer to the&nbsp;<a href="/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-after-you-are-nominated">How to request a 205(a) Letter of Support</a>&nbsp;for more information on the process and fee payment requirements.</li></ul><p>There is no guarantee that AAIP will extend your Business Performance Agreement term length or will issue you a 205(a) Letter of Support to extend your work permit. In addition, the issuance of the 205(a) Letter of Support by AAIP does not guarantee that IRCC will extend your work permit.</p>

  </div>

  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h3>Step 7. Final Report for Nomination</h3><p>Once candidates satisfy all the conditions of the Business Performance Agreement, they can apply to be nominated by AAIP for permanent residency. Candidates must submit a <a href="https://cfr.forms.gov.ab.ca/form/AINP12705" target="_blank">Final Report for Nomination</a> along with the supporting business documents.</p><p>Alberta retains the sole discretion to determine that the candidate is deemed to have fulfilled and satisfied the obligations in the Business Performance Agreement, including the job creation requirements.</p><h4>If your Final Report for Nomination&nbsp;is approved</h4><p>If your Final Report for Nomination is approved, AAIP will:</p><ul><li>notify Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) of your nomination</li><li>send a nomination letter to you explaining how to submit your permanent residency application to the IRCC Centralized Intake Office (CIO). You must apply for permanent residence to IRCC.</li></ul><p>You must continue to meet the conditions of your Business Performance Agreement during AAIP nomination assessment. If you no longer meet the conditions of your Business Performance Agreement, you will not be issued a nomination certificate and you will be declined.</p><p>You may use your nomination to renew your work permit.</p><p>A one-time nomination extension may be issued under the Rural Entrepreneur Stream if all nomination extension requirements are met. Request for a nomination extension is subject to a processing fee of $150.</p><p>IRCC will contact you for further clarification or additional documents for your Application for Permanent Residence. If IRCC approves your application, you and your dependents will receive a permanent resident visa from IRCC.</p><p>Learn more about what to do <a href="/aaip-rural-entrepreneur-stream-after-you-are-nominated">after you are nominated</a>.</p><h4>If your Final Report for Nomination is declined</h4><p>If your Final Report for Nomination is declined, you will be emailed a decision letter providing the reasons your Business Application was declined. If you identified an immigration representative to act on your behalf for your Business Application, the decision email will be sent to you and copied to your representative.</p><p>If you can demonstrate an error was made in assessing your Business Application, you may be eligible to submit a&nbsp;Request for Reconsideration.</p>

  </div>


  </div>

  

  
  <div  class="goa-main-grid" >
    
  
  <div class="paragraph paragraph--type--text-block paragraph--view-mode--default goa-column-100-100-100">
    
  <h2>Request for reconsideration</h2><p>All requests for reconsideration are subject to a service fee of $250. Information on how to pay the fee is provided below.</p><p>You may only submit one request for reconsideration for each decision that you receive from <abbr title="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program">AAIP</abbr> on your Business Application.</p><p>Requests will not be processed until full payment is received.</p><h3>Eligibility for reconsideration</h3><p>You may submit a request for reconsideration on the decision of your&nbsp;Business Application only if you meet the following criteria.</p><p>A request for reconsideration must:</p><ul><li>be directly related to the reason(s) stated in the decision letter</li><li>demonstrate that an error was made in applying program criteria to the Business Application or Final Report for Nomination</li><li>be submitted by:<ul><li>the candidate named on the decision letter, or</li><li>a representative of the candidate</li></ul></li><li>be submitted within 30 calendar days of the date the decision letter was issued</li></ul><p>A request for reconsideration cannot be used:</p><ul><li>to ask for an exemption or exception to program criteria</li><li>as an opportunity to submit new evidence or re-state the evidence submitted in the Business Application or Final Report for Nomination</li><li>if you are unsatisfied with the final decision or a component of the final decision</li><li>to request Humanitarian and Compassionate consideration</li></ul><h3>How to submit a request for reconsideration</h3><h4>Step 1. Complete the online request</h4><p><a class="goa-cta" href="https://aaip.labour.alberta.ca/">AAIP online portal</a></p><ul><li>Log into the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program portal.</li><li>On the AAIP portal main page, click on 'Create New Request'.</li><li>Find the AAIP Business Application number that you want to submit a request for reconsideration. Only Business Applications with a status of 'Declined' or 'Withdrawn' are eligible for reconsideration.</li><li>Once you click on the Business Application number, you will be directed to a page with detailed information. You can only see request options that are available to you for this Business Application. If you are eligible to submit a request for reconsideration for the Business Application, a blue 'Request for Reconsideration' button will be available above the Business Application number.</li><li>Click on the blue 'Request for Reconsideration' button.</li><li>Next, read the <a href="https://open.alberta.ca/publications/p28p5" target="_blank"><abbr title="Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy">POPA clause</abbr></a> and other important information about completing a request. Check off the box and click on Continue. A draft is automatically saved once you click on the Continue button. If you experience any technical issues or leave the request section while creating the request, you will need to go to the&nbsp;'Check Existing Requests'&nbsp;page to retrieve the draft request to continue.</li><li>Complete the online declaration and upload supporting document(s), if applicable, and click on 'Submit Request' button.</li><li>Next, you must pay the fee.</li></ul><h4>Step 2. Pay the fee</h4><p>All requests for reconsideration are subject to a service fee of $250.</p><p><strong>Fee conditions</strong></p><p>The AAIP request for reconsideration fee will automatically be refunded by AAIP only if the original decision on your Business Application is overturned. Other refunds for requests for reconsideration fees will not be considered.</p><p><strong>Online payment</strong></p><p>Instructions on how to pay your fee are available in the portal. For any online fee payment issues, contact <a href="https://eservices.alberta.ca/contact/" target="_blank">eServices</a>.</p><p>You will need to pay your fee in full within 24 hours of submitting your request in the portal. If you do not pay your fee within 24 hours of submitting your request, your request will be cancelled. Cancelled requests cannot be reinstated and a new request must be created and submitted.</p><p>Check the Request Status on the Check Existing Requests page to confirm if your request fee has been paid. Your fee has not been paid if the status is 'Payment Pending.' Your request has been accepted for processing by AAIP if the status is 'Submitted'. You will receive an email with your fee payment receipt.</p><h3>After you submit a request for reconsideration</h3><p>You can check the online portal for the status of your request.</p><p>The information you submitted will be reviewed and if the request meets the required conditions, a Program Officer with AAIP who was not involved in the original decision will conduct a review of the decision on your Business Application.</p><p>There is no guarantee that a request for reconsideration will result in your Business Application being re-assessed.</p><p><strong>Note</strong>: You will be notified by email once a decision has been rendered on your request for reconsideration.&nbsp;</p><h4>If you do not qualify to request reconsideration</h4><p>If you do not meet the conditions listed above to request reconsideration, you may wish to review the AAIP website for information and&nbsp;updates to AAIP criteria, to determine if you meet the eligibility criteria to re-apply.</p><h2>Resources</h2><ul><li><a href="/system/files/im-aaip-business-plan-guidelines.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="dcd86d96-68c7-4da4-8c10-4282daa4ea15"
