NPRT 1001, NPRT 2101, and NPRT 2102 are clinical courses that require in-person attendance for four weeks at a time.
Edmonton, Wetaskwin: Clinical shifts are Monday to Friday with a mix of day (6:30 am to 2:30 pm) and evening (2:30 pm to 10:30 pm) schedule.
Mobile Castor or Killam lab: These will be 12-hour day shifts on the weekend (6:30 am to 6:30 pm).
NPRT 2200  is an eight-week comprehensive clinical. This clinical may be provided within a requested area, depending on site availability. Students will work the same shifts as the ones listed above. This may include weekends, evenings, nights, holidays, or a combination of shifts.
