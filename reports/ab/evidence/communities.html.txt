  <h2>Overview</h2><p>The Rural Renewal Stream is one of several immigration streams to address local labour needs and skill shortages in rural Alberta communities and helps newcomers settle into the community. This stream requires a community to apply to the Government of Alberta for community designation.</p><p>This stream is both voluntary and community driven. Once designated, the community then works with employers to attract, recruit and retain newcomers by sharing information on settlement supports. This may include accommodation, education, health care and services such as language training.</p><p>Designated communities will each receive a yearly allocation from which they are able to recruit and endorse candidates to fill their local labour needs. A candidate must be selected and endorsed by a designated community and meet all criteria for the Rural Renewal Stream.</p><p>Candidates should not apply to the province of Alberta if they do not have an endorsement of candidate letter from a designated community.</p><h2>Designated communities</h2><h3>List of current designated communities</h3><p><strong>Table 1.</strong> List of designated fill out a Community Designation Application form. Additional points will be awarded for communities with less than 10,000 residents. Once a community is designated, they will be able to attract, recruit and retain newcomers using this stream.</p><h3>Mandatory requirements</h3><p>An application will only be evaluated if all of the mandatory requirements below are met:</p><ul><li>rural communities with a population less than 100,000<ul><li>rural communities in the same economic region may form a partnership to submit an application</li><li>annual <a href="https://open.alberta.ca/publications/2368-7320" target="_blank">population figures</a> will be used to determine population count (population figures will be reviewed annually)</li><li>communities must fall outside of the <a href="https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2016/as-sa/fogs-spg/Facts-cma-eng.cfm?LANG=eng&amp;GK=CMA&amp;GC=825" target="_blank">Calgary</a>&nbsp;and <a href="https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2016/as-sa/fogs-spg/Facts-cma-eng.cfm?LANG=Eng&amp;GK=CMA&amp;GC=835" target="_blank">Edmonton</a> census metropolitan area</li></ul></li><li>one or more employers are interested in participating and have permanent (minimum 12 months), full-time, non-seasonal employment available</li><li>an endorsement letter of support from participating town or municipal councils signed by the mayor or reeve</li><li>application is signed and submitted by the economic development organization with a primary function in economic development<ul><li>includes an economic development plan or similar document (does not need to be current)</li></ul></li></ul><h3>Uses not allowed</h3><p>The Rural Renewal Stream cannot be used for:</p><ul><li>recruiting part-time or seasonal workers</li><li>individuals nominated under the stream will still need to be licensed by the respective Provincial Regulatory Organization (PRO) if their occupation is regulated or meet Alberta industry trade standards for a specific trade in Alberta<ul><li>Learn more about <a href="/foreign-qualification-recognition">regulated occupations in Alberta</a></li><li>Learn more about <a href="https://tradesecrets.alberta.ca/trades-occupations/" target="_blank">industry trade standards in Alberta</a></li></ul></li><li>permanent residency approval is still required by the Government of Canada</li></ul><h2>How to apply</h2><h3>Before you apply</h3><p>Communities must have the capacity to attract, recruit and welcome newcomers in order to participate in the Rural Renewal Stream. A Rural Renewal Stream candidate is a foreign national who does not have permanent residence or Canadian citizenship. This person could be living in or outside of Canada.</p><p>Read the <a href="/system/files/im-rural-renewal-stream-fact-sheet.pdf" target="_blank" data-entity-type="media" data-entity-uuid="15445573-09f9-4d05-a8ca-5f23415a8931" data-entity-substitution="media" title="im-rural-renewal-stream-fact-sheet.pdf">Rural Renewal Stream Factsheet</a>.</p><h3>Community requirements</h3><p>Communities will be responsible for:</p><ul><li>identifying and working with community employers that have permanent (minimum of 12 months, full-time, non-seasonal) jobs that need to be filled</li><li>connecting with a settlement providing organizations to identify and plan for some of the settlement needs within the community</li><li>​developing additional criteria (optional) to recruit foreign nationals<ul><li>this could include narrowing down the occupations that the community is interested in recruiting</li></ul></li><li>responding to foreign national inquiries while community is actively recruiting</li><li>collaborating with employers on selecting and endorsing the foreign national</li><li>developing and implementing a plan for welcoming and settling foreign national(s) to the community</li></ul><p>There is no direct funding for the Rural Renewal Stream. However, the Government of Alberta offers grants to support capacity building of small centre or rural areas to attract, welcome and retain newcomers in rural Alberta. These grants are part of an annual call for proposals that support community capacity building.</p><p><a href="/alberta-settlement-and-integration-program">Learn more about these grants</a></p><h3>Complete the application form</h3><p>An economic development organization fills out the application in partnership with the community.</p><ul><li><a href="https://cfr.forms.gov.ab.ca/form/AINP12963" target="_blank">Rural Renewal Community Designation Application form</a></li></ul><p><strong>Note</strong>: Incomplete applications and/or applications that do not have all of the mandatory requirements will not be evaluated.</p><h4>Designation support letter from participating town or municipal councils signed by the mayor or reeve</h4><p>The letter should outline the following on the official letterhead:</p><ul><li>name and address of the participating town or municipality supporting the application</li><li>participating town/municipality acknowledges support for this project and the meeting date that this project was discussed</li><li>participating town or municipality acknowledges that their community name will be posted on the Government of Alberta website should their community become designated</li><li>name, title and contact information of the mayor or reeve issuing the designation support letter</li></ul><h3>Submit the application</h3><p>The completed application and supporting documents need to be submitted for designation by email to <a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d5b4b4bca5bab3b3bcb6b095b2baa3fbb4b7fbb6b4"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="a6c7c7cfd6c9c0c0cfc5c3e6c1c9d088c7c488c5c7">[email&#160;protected]</span></a>.</p><p>Applications will be evaluated on a points system (points for each category are noted in the application). Communities must score a minimum of 150 points in order to be considered for designation. In the event that multiple applications are received at the same time, selection will be based on top scores.</p><h3>After you have submitted</h3><p>Albera Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) representatives will review the initial application and let the applicant know if the application is complete (all sections of the application are fully completed and all mandatories have been provided or met). The amount of time this review will take will depend on the volume of applications.</p><p>Once the application has been reviewed and deemed complete, the applicant will receive an email confirming the official date of submission. At this point, the application cannot be altered.</p><p>The application will be evaluated and an AAIP representative will contact the community to indicate the status of the application within 8 weeks of the official submission date. The applicant will be notified if more time is needed and will be notified of the outcome of the application via email.</p><p>Community designation will last 3 years to the date that the community received email confirmation of designation.&nbsp;</p><p>There will be an option to extend the designation by an additional 2 years to a maximum of 5 years. Extension information will be provided to communities as required.</p><h2>After receiving designation</h2><h3>Attract and recruit</h3><p>A designated community, in partnership with their local economic development organization and participating employers, are responsible for recruiting foreign nationals. Candidates may be recruited from within Alberta, Canada or outside of Canada.</p><p>Candidates that are currently living in Canada are only eligible if they have legal status in Canada. If the community is recruiting outside Canada, all IRCC immigration rules and regulations apply. The Government of Alberta will not compensate the community for any recruitment costs.</p><p>The community partnership reviews candidate applications to ensure candidates meet all criteria, including the <a href="/aaip-rural-renewal-stream-eligibility">program eligibility</a>. Communities may choose to have additional criteria separate from the mandatory criteria. This can include a particular skill set or certain occupations that help the community fill a specific labour need.</p><p>The employer provides a job offer to the successful candidate and the community endorses the newcomer.</p><h3>Select and endorse newcomers</h3><p>All candidates must have an Endorsement of Candidate letter from their Designated Community. Endorsement of Candidate letter is only valid for one year from the date of issuance.</p><h4>Content for the Endorsement of Candidate letter</h4><p>Endorsement letters are valid for one year from the date of issuance. The letter must be on the official letterhead and issued by the local economic development organization of the community and must outline the following:</p><ul><li>date of issuance</li><li>name and address of the economic development organization supporting the application</li><li>candidate’s full name and residential address</li><li>details of the bona fide job offer:<ul><li>name of the business</li><li>name and contact information of the employer</li><li>location of employment</li><li>position and description of job duties</li><li>duration of employment and work conditions</li></ul></li><li>detailed description of how filling the position offered aligns with the economic growth of the designated community</li><li>the employer’s efforts to fill the position within Canada and outcomes</li><li>name, title and contact information of the person issuing the endorsement of the candidate</li></ul><h4>Permanent residency</h4><ul><li>Candidate applies to AAIP.</li><li>The candidate submits their application through the AAIP Portal ensuring all criteria for Rural Renewal Stream is met.</li><li>Nominee applies to Immigration Refugees and Citizenship Canada for permanent residency.</li><li>The Government of Canada makes the final decision on permanent residency applications. A nomination by the province does not guarantee an approved permanent residency application.</li></ul><h3>Welcome and integrate newcomers</h3><p>It is the responsibility of the community to identify what kind of settlement supports they will offer, develop and implement a settlement plan to help the newcomer and their family settle into the community.</p><ul><li><a href="http://www.auma.ca/advocacy-services/programs-initiatives/welcoming-and-inclusive-communities" target="_blank">Alberta Municipalities</a> has resources around welcoming and inclusive communities.</li><li><a href="https://aaisa.ca/" target="_blank">Alberta Association of Immigrant Serving Agencies</a> has a list of immigration serving organizations.</li></ul><p>Pre-arrival settlement services are available through the Government of Canada for permanent residents. The province has some services available for nominated temporary residents in Canada with a valid work permit.</p><ul><li><a href="/make-alberta-your-home">Make Alberta your home</a></li></ul><h3>Community evaluation</h3><p>An evaluation of the community designation may be conducted at the end of the designation period. The community will need to participate in the evaluation by providing information as requested.</p><p>Communities interested in extending their designation will be provided information on the process.</p><h2>Contact</h2><p>Connect with us if you have questions about community designation:<br>Email: <a href="/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3253535b425d54545b515772555d441c53501c5153"><span class="__cf_email__" data-cfemail="2d4c4c445d424b4b444e486d4a425b034c4f034e4c">[email&#160;protected]</span></a></p>
